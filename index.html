ModuleDescription
Dynalisp ModuleThe Dynalisp module groups the in-house DynALisp programs: drawing and hatching tools, layer standards (creation, phases, filters, transfers), drawing units, grids and scales, environment setup, general utilities and the diagnostics that find what goes wrong.
LeeMac Sample ModuleThe LeeMac module is a sample module: it showcases what DynALisp does with a collection of existing AutoLISP programs, here programs by Lee Mac (www.lee-mac.com).
