Revisions

The developer docs of Revisions: its files and how its code works. What it does and how to use it are in its User Guide page.

Source FilesModules\Dynalisp\Remote\Dyl-Revisions.lsp (the project, its files, the numbers, the update of the sheets, loaded at startup), Modules\Dynalisp\Apps\Output\Dyl-Revisions.lsp (the windows, the clouds and the commands), Modules\Dynalisp\Res\Dyl-Revisions.xaml, Modules\Dynalisp\Res\Dyl-RevisionTitleBlocks.xaml (the windows)
DataThe DynALisp folder of the project (below); Res\RevisionBlocks.csv (the title blocks, your copy under User); in each layout the XRecord DYL_REVISIONS and the XData DYL_REV of the clouds and tags; the user setting DynalispRevisionSync
FollowsThe Project Files convention

Where The Revisions Are Kept

The revisions are project data, shared by everyone who works on the project: they are kept in the project itself, in its DynALisp folder beside the .apj, never in your DynALisp settings.

FileWhat it holds
Revisions.csvThe revisions, in their sequence.
RevisionSettings.csvThe numbering and the first number of the project.
Sheets.csvEvery sheet, the revisions given to it and the ones its clouds had at its last update.

Each layout also keeps a copy of the revisions given to it: a sheet drawing copied or renamed keeps its revisions. When another user saves the revisions or the settings of the project while your window is open, OK and Update All Sheets are refused: cancel, then open the window again.

The formats, divided by ;, in UTF-8 when AutoCAD can write it:

Revisions.csv         Id;Numbering;Date;Description;IssuedTo;IssuedBy;Issued
RevisionSettings.csv  Setting;Value        (Numbering: Project|Sheet, Start: 1|0, LastId)
Sheets.csv            File;Layout;Revisions;Clouds   (File relative to the project, ids by |)
RevisionBlocks.csv    Block;Rows;First;Number;Date;Description;IssuedTo;IssuedBy;Current;CurrentDate

The Project

  • Remote>>Dyl:RevProject walks up from the folder of the drawing to the first one holding a .apj, and reads in it the name, the number and SheetLocation (Remote>>Dyl:RevXmlText, RevXmlAtt). Remote>>Dyl:RevProjectOf does it for the current drawing; the result is a list whose 5th item is the sheet folder and 6th the DynALisp folder (Remote>>Dyl:RevPath).
  • Remote>>Dyl:RevSheetFiles lists the drawings of the sheet folder and its sub-folders; RevRel and RevFull turn a path relative to the project and back.

A Revision And Its Number

  • A revision is a list of strings, (id numbering date description issuedto issuedby issued): the id is never given again (LastId), the numbering one of *rev:numberings*, issued "1" or "".
  • (Remote>>Dyl:RevNumbers revs start) numbers a list in its sequence, each numbering counting on its own: Numeric from start, Alphabetic through Remote>>Dyl:RevAlpha (A…Z, AA...), None "". It returns ((id . number) ...).
  • Remote>>Dyl:RevSheetNumbers gives the numbers of a sheet: per project, the numbers of the whole list; per sheet, RevNumbers of the revisions the sheet has (Remote>>Dyl:RevOn).

What A Sheet Has

A layout has a revision given to it (the Revisions column of Sheets.csv, copied in its XRecord, Remote>>Dyl:RevXrecGet / RevXrecPut: (1 . "ids") (70 . flag)), or the one of a cloud drawn on it. (Remote>>Dyl:RevScan block maps) reads the paper space block of a layout once and returns (clouds tags blocks): the ids of the polylines and tags with the XData DYL_REV (Remote>>Dyl:RevXData: id and kind), and the block references whose name matches a title block (Remote>>Dyl:RevBlockMap: the name itself first, then wcmatch; the effective name of a dynamic block).

Updating A Drawing, Step By Step

  1. (Remote>>Dyl:RevUpdateFiles prj files) reads the revisions, the settings, the title blocks and Sheets.csv once, then for each drawing:
    • open in this AutoCAD (Remote>>Dyl:RevOpenDoc): updated through its document, not saved; another open drawing that refuses the change is listed (run Pst-RevisionsUpdate in it);
    • locked by another user (Remote>>Dyl:RevLockedBy reads its .dwl): skipped and listed;
    • closed: opened in ObjectDBX.AxDbDocument.<version>, updated, and saved with vla-SaveAs only when a value changed.
    A progress bar shows with the Express Tools (acet-ui-progress).
  2. Remote>>Dyl:RevSyncDoc works on each layout: its revisions (the row of Sheets.csv, else its XRecord, plus its clouds), their numbers, then each title block is filled (Remote>>Dyl:RevFill) and each tag gets its number. Remote>>Dyl:RevPut writes an attribute only when its text differs, and returns the count of values written.
  3. ObjectDBX does not move an attribute that is not left-justified to its new text: RevPut returns 100 or more for it, the layout gets flag 1 in its XRecord, and the next update in an open drawing realigns it (Remote>>Dyl:RevRealign).
  4. The rows of the drawings updated are merged into Sheets.csv, read again just before it is written (Remote>>Dyl:RevSheetsMerge): only their rows are replaced, so another user's update is kept.
  5. It returns ((file status) ...): Updated, Up to date, Updated, not saved, Open by <user> or an error.

Remote>>Dyl:RevisionsWatch, a startup step of each drawing while DynalispRevisionSync is on, runs Remote>>Dyl:RevUpdateThis when the drawing is a sheet of a project that has Revisions.csv; REVUP runs it by hand.

The Revisions Window

  • c:Dyl-Revisions reads the project and keeps its locals: rv:revs, rv:sets, rv:shts and their copies as read (rv:revs0...), the selections rv:sel and rv:ssel, and rv:stamp, the times of Revisions.csv and RevisionSettings.csv (Dyl:RevStamps).
  • Dyl:RevSheetsOf reads the sheet drawings Sheets.csv does not have yet (Remote>>Dyl:RevReadFiles, without changing them).
  • The window, Res\Dyl-Revisions.xaml, is shown by Dyl:WinShow with the values of Dyl:RevWinVals. Its revisions are a list of rows edited in the window (rev/rows: the columns of each revision, then its sequence and its number, shown, Dyl:RevRow): a field changed goes to the revisions selected that are not issued (the rule !iss read on each row). Its sheets (Dyl:RevSheetLine) follow the revision picked: sht/by holds the items of the sheets for each revision, shh/by and scn/by the heading and the count (dyl:Win.ItemsBy).
  • What changes the numbers closes the window with its number (see the window file): c:Dyl-Revisions reads the revisions back (Dyl:RevWinRevs) and the selections and the numbering of the project (Dyl:RevWinGet), does the work and shows it again with its message: Dyl:RevNew, Dyl:RevDelete (its question asked between two windows, Dyl:Ask), Dyl:RevMove, Dyl:RevSheetsPut. They refuse an issued revision, a move past an issued one, and a delete when Dyl:RevKeepsIssued-p finds that an issued revision would get another number; a numbering picked (5) is refused for the same reason, the revisions keeping theirs.
  • OK and Update All Sheets run Dyl:RevCheck first: refused when a stamp changed since the window opened (compared with equal: they are lists), or a revision has no description. Dyl:RevSave writes only what changed; Dyl:RevToUpdate keeps the sheets whose title block would show something else (Dyl:RevRender before and after), or all of them after the title blocks changed.
  • The Revision Title Blocks window (Dyl:RevBlocksDialog, Res\Dyl-RevisionTitleBlocks.xaml, a list of rows edited in the window: tbl/rows, read back by Dyl:RevBlocksFix and checked again by Dyl:RevBlocksCheck) edits RevisionBlocks.csv; Pick... closes it, Dyl:RevBlocksGuess reads the tags of the block picked (a numbered tag that comes for several numbers is a row, Dyl:RevGuess its column from its words, English and French), and the window opens again.

Revision Clouds

c:Pst-RevisionCloud works in the paper space of a layout only. Dyl:RevCloudOf lists the revisions not issued and asks for a sequence; REVCLOUD draws on the layer of the ANNREV layer key; the new polyline gets the XData (DYL_REV id "Cloud") (Dyl:RevMark); the tag block DYL-REVTAG is made the first time (Dyl:RevTagDef: a triangle 8 mm wide, 5/16" in an imperial drawing, its attribute REV), inserted and marked "Tag"; then the drawing is updated. An error handler sets the current layer back when Escape stops REVCLOUD.

Using It In An App

CallReturns
(Remote>>Dyl:RevProjectOf)The project of the current drawing, nil when it is in none.
(Remote>>Dyl:RevRead prj)The revisions of the project, in their sequence.
(Remote>>Dyl:RevUpdateFiles prj files)Updates the sheet drawings, returns their status.

Dyl-Diagnostics checks the project in its Drawing section (dg:dwg-revisions): its files, its sheet folder, and each layout with revisions but no title block set up.

Known Limitations

  • A cloud counts only in the paper space of its layout.
  • A drawing saved through ObjectDBX takes the format of this AutoCAD and may lose its preview image.
  • A title block in an xref, or filled by sheet set fields, is not changed.
