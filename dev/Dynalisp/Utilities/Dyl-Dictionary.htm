Dictionary

Definitions File

Res\Dictionaries\<code>-def.csv, UTF-8, one word per row: Word;Forms;Type;Category;Subcategory;Definition;See also.

  • Word: the word as written in the custom dictionary (singular, masculine), or an expression of several words. Expressions are only in the definitions file: the spell checker checks each of their words separately.
  • Forms: its other forms in the custom dictionary (plural, feminine, other spellings), separated by commas. They are searched with the word.
  • Type: n.m., n.f., adj., v., sigle, abr., n.pr., num. (Roman numeral) or, for an expression, loc.n.m., loc.n.f., loc.adj. or loc.adv.; several separated by / (n.m./adj.).
  • Category and Subcategory: the theme of the word, in 10 categories: Structure, Enveloppe, Intérieur, Mécanique et électricité, Matériaux, Architecture, Patrimoine et rénovation, Projet et réglementation, Abréviations et sigles, Mots composés (Structure > Pièce sur pièce, Enveloppe > Ferblanterie, Architecture > Églises : mobilier liturgique...).
  • Definition: one or two sentences, without the ; separator.
  • See also: related words of the file, separated by commas.

A word added to the custom dictionary from AutoCAD (Add to dictionary) is not added to the definitions file: add its row with Edit list....

About 100 terms defined by the Quebec Construction Code (National Building Code as amended for Quebec, Division A, Article 1.4.1.2), such as mur coupe-feu, premier étage or distance limitative, end their definition with Terme défini du code.: tick Also in the definitions and search terme defini to list them all. The ones not found elsewhere are in Projet et réglementation > Termes définis du code. Their definitions are reworded summaries, not the text of the code: check the code before relying on one.

The Code

  • The files – dic:dir and dic:files find the definitions files of Res\Dictionaries, dic:code the one of the language of the file preferences; dic:read reads one in UTF-8 (dic:open), the fields with dic:split.
  • The search – dic:fold drops accents, ligatures and case from a word, so dic:match-p compares folded words: every word typed must be in the word or one of its forms. dic:types and dic:distinct fill the filters, dic:dlgsubs the subcategories of a category.
  • The window – dic:dlgload, dic:dlglist, dic:dlgshow (the word selected, its text wrapped by dic:wrap), dic:dlgsearch, dic:dlgjump (Go to), dic:dlgclear.
  • Copy word – dic:clip puts the word on the clipboard through the htmlfile object of Windows; Edit list... makes the user copy (Dyl:UserFileW) and opens it with dic:shell.
