Ribbon

The developer docs of Ribbon: its files and how its code works. What it does and how to use it are in its User Guide page.

Building The Ribbon

For whoever changes DynALisp: the tab is generated, never edited in the CUI editor.

  • Installer\Ribbon\BuildRibbon.py writes DynALisp.cuix from Installer\Ribbon\Ribbon.csv (the panels and buttons), RibbonIcons.py (the icons) and Resources\StartupSuite.csv (the names, descriptions and children). It warns about a main command missing from the ribbon or without an icon. --sheet <file.png> draws every icon on the light and the dark ribbon.
  • The texts of the tab in another language are in Installer\Ribbon\Ribbon.fr.csv (a panel, a command or an item of the Quick Draw gallery, its name and its tooltip); the script writes them by the id of each item in Resources\DynALisp.Ribbon.fr.csv, which the plugin reads, and warns about a text missing.
  • The drop-downs are built with dotnet build Installer\Ribbon\Plugin -c Release, AutoCAD closed.
  • The same run draws the pictures of this page from the same panels and icons (Installer\Ribbon\RibbonPanels.py, into Docs\Plugins\Ribbon) and writes its Panels section: they follow the ribbon, with no screenshot to take. --pictures draws them only.
  • Restart AutoCAD to see the new ribbon.

The Plugin

  • Plugin.cs – the IExtensionApplication of the DLL, its [assembly: CommandClass(...)] listing every class that holds a LispFunction (a static class cannot be listed, and a function of a class not listed is never defined, so every (if Dyl:X ...) of the Lisp side does nothing). Host holds what every part shares: Host.OnIdle (the ribbon is changed only when AutoCAD is idle with no command running), Host.Picks (only an item the user picks runs a setter), Host.Standard (the AEC lists of Res\AEC\Standard), Host.Labels, Host.Run. Part is the base of a drop-down part, Part.Later asks for a change at the next idle.
  • PhaseCombos.cs – the Auto-phase and Phase drop-downs: Dyl:RibbonPhase records the state the Lisp side pushes (Remote>>Dyl:PhaseStatus), each drawing keeps its own, and a pick runs Remote>>Dyl:PhaseSet or PhaseAutoSet (see Layer Phase).
  • TransferCombos.cs – the Level, From and To drop-downs of Layer Transfer, their lists read from the drawing after each command; Dyl:RibbonTransfer gives the choice to Pst-TransferField. NcsName reads a layer name with the rule of Dyl-LayTransfer-Ncs: change both together.
  • Translations.cs – the tab in the language of the AutoCAD installed: the texts of DynALisp.Ribbon.<code>.csv put on the items by their UID, again when AutoCAD makes the ribbon again; Dyl:RibbonLang and Dyl:RibbonLangInfo.
  • Skin.cs – an experiment, not supported by Autodesk: the ribbon and the palettes of AutoCAD in the dark of the DynALisp windows, while Display is on and its Interface is DynALisp or DynALisp Neutral in Dyl-Settings (a Look each: the hue and saturation the greys take and how much darker they get, 217° 10–16 % ×0.78 for DynALisp, no hue ×0.62 for DynALisp Neutral; both targets, the native themes, the web pages and the hosted themes take the look of the Interface, and another look chosen gives the colours back before putting them in the new one) (DynalispDisplayOn, DynalispInterface, or while it is not set the former DynalispPaletteColors and DynalispRibbonColors, read from the registry when the plugin loads) and AutoCAD is in its dark theme (COLORTHEME 0). Each is a target (SkinTarget), a theme object of AutoCAD: the ribbon of AdWindows.dll is bound to the brushes of ComponentManager.CurrentTheme (Autodesk.Internal.Windows.Theme: Ribbon, Ribbon.MainTab, ApplicationMenu, ApplicationFrame...); the palettes, the status bar, the file tabs and the command line take theirs from ThemeManager.PaletteSettings.CurrentTheme of AdUiPalettes.dll (Autodesk.Windows.Palettes.PaletteTheme: PaletteContainerBackgroundColor, PropertyInspector*, StatusBar*, CommandLine*... some 700 colours). Their Brush and Color properties, walked by reflection, go through a filter (Skin.Map): a grey or a blue-grey (saturation up to 35 %) takes the hue of the windows (217°, 10 to 16 %), a dark one 22 % darker; a saturated colour, an image brush and the icons stay. The theme is put in a copy made current, so AutoCAD tells every part drawn from it (the title bar of AutoCAD, AdImpApplicationFrame, keeps its own copy of the colours, read when a theme is made current), the original made current again to give them back; when AutoCAD takes the copy back twice (it makes another theme current), the theme is changed in place when it is not frozen (what it was is kept to give it back). Skin.OnIdle, run by Host.OnIdle once a second at most, puts it on again when AutoCAD makes another theme current (COLORTHEME changed, the ribbon made again) and takes it off when it is turned off. (Dyl:RibbonSkin) ((Dyl:RibbonSkin "Palettes") for the palettes) reads the settings again and returns what it does (DynALisp (57 colours), off, waiting for the dark theme, not found: ...); Dyl-Diagnostics reports it (Conflicts). Anything not found leaves the ribbon as it is. The contextual tabs keep their colours (their own RibbonTab.Theme).
  • NativeThemes.cs – with Palettes, the palettes written in C++ (Properties and its property inspector), which draw with the native themes of AdUi (CAdUiTheme of the CAdUiThemeManager of the ObjectARX headers, one colour per AdUiThemeElement), out of reach of .NET. Found in AutoCAD itself, once: the functions in the export tables of the modules named adui*, by their full C++ names (?GetTheme@CAdUiThemeManager@@QEAAPEAVCAdUiTheme@@PEB_W@Z, GetColor, SetColor, HasTheme, ReleaseTheme, IsDark), which give their exact arguments: a name not exactly the one expected, or not one function returning the manager, and nothing is called. The themes are asked by the names found in AutoCAD 2026 (ACTIVETHEME, PaletteSet, ToolPalette, PropertyPalette...) made with GetTheme when not there yet (PropertyPalette, before Properties first opens), so a palette finds its theme in the colours of DynALisp and never shows those of AutoCAD first. The colours are first applied as the plugin loads, then at each idle second, even while a command or the startup of a drawing runs (they change no part of the ribbon). When none of them is there (another version), and with (Dyl:RibbonSkin "Native" "Search"), every word (UTF-16 and ASCII) of the read-only data of the modules of the AutoCAD folder is asked with HasTheme (a lookup that creates nothing): about a second. Each dark theme (IsDark, or a dark kPaletteBackground) has its colours, elements 8 to 199 (kPaletteBackground to the frame; the fonts before), put through Skin.Map with SetColor, between SuspendModifiedNotifications and ResumeModifiedNotifications, then SendModifiedNotification tells the palettes to draw again, once per theme (each SetColor alone redraws them, and they flickered: those three are used only when exported); what each was is kept and given back when Palettes is turned off. Several names give the same theme, and themes share colours: a theme is filtered once, a colour already given never again (Skin.Map darkens each time, and the colours flipped between the two), and black or almost (a transparent colour) is left. A colour that DynALisp did not give (AutoCAD changed its theme) makes its theme filtered again; a theme AutoCAD sets again more than 3 times is given back and left alone, and so is the managed theme of a target AutoCAD makes current again more than 5 times, since they would flicker. (Dyl:RibbonSkin "Palettes") names the themes changed, or what was not found; (Dyl:RibbonSkin "Native") lists, one string each, how the names were found (the words, modules and time of a search), then each theme with its module, dark or light, its background and the colours changed, or the name whose theme it is, and each theme set again, how many times and the last colour found; with "Exports", the functions of CAdUiTheme exported.
  • Hosted.cs – with the Interface, the parts of AutoCAD made in WPF that draw with a theme object of their own, made once from those of the targets, and kept the blue-greys of AutoCAD: the file tabs (FileTabsColor of AcWindows.dll, a ThemeBase), a palette given its own theme (ThemeManager.OverridePaletteTheme). Each WPF window of AutoCAD (PresentationSource.CurrentSources) is walked: a theme is an Autodesk Freezable (not a brush, an image or a geometry) that is the source of a binding of a colour (Background, BorderBrush, Foreground, Fill, Stroke), the data context or a resource of an element, or its OverridePaletteTheme, never a theme of a target or a part of one (filtered there). Each one found unfrozen goes through Skin.Filter, what it had kept to give it back; a theme whose colours its part set again (a drawing opened) is filtered again, 3 times at most. A window is walked when it first shows, all of them again every 30 s at most when their number changed. The file tabs and the layout tabs (SwitchContentControl) get their colour from the code of AutoCAD, a local value of Background set again as their state changes (current, under the mouse): a ValueChanged handler puts each colour it sets in the look at once, what it set kept to give it back, and the lists of tabs (SwitchItemsControl) are watched, so a tab added is found at once. (Dyl:RibbonSkin "Hosted") writes to %TEMP%\DynALisp-Hosted.txt the themes found, each WPF element still drawn in a blue-grey of AutoCAD (215–220°, as #222933, #3B4453, #4E5A6E) with where its colour comes from, each window of AutoCAD with such pixels on the screen (its class and those of its parents, its size, its colours, WPF or not), the elements of the native themes that had those colours, the functions exported about the application frame (the title bar is drawn in C++ by AdImpApplicationFrame) and the report of the web pages.
  • WebPages.cs – with Palettes, the web pages of AutoCAD (the Start tab), drawn by their own style sheets. In AutoCAD 2027 their browser (WebView2) is made in C++ by AcWebView.dll: its functions are found in its export table by their full C++ names, as in NativeThemes.cs (AcBrowserEngineProxy::Instance, newWindowIterator, findBrowserWindowByName, findBrowserWindowByUrl, AcBrowserWindowProxy::executeScript, name, url), its browser windows listed (by an iterator it makes, ended but not freed: listed again only when the Chrome_WidgetWin_0 windows of AutoCAD change, looked at every second, and every 30 s) and found again by their name, or their page when they have none, and the script run in each every 150 ms, by a DispatcherTimer (it does nothing a second time in the same page: a page loaded again gets it again, and shows its own colours that long at most). When a managed browser control shows them (WebView2, in WPF or Windows Forms, or CefSharp; found by its type name in the visual trees and the child windows of AutoCAD, every 2 s while a browser assembly is loaded, and used only by reflection), a script is run in its pages: the colours of each element (background, text, borders) through the filter of Skin.Map, written in JavaScript, and those of the elements added after (a MutationObserver); with WebView2 it is added to the pages opened next too (AddScriptToExecuteOnDocumentCreatedAsync). Turning Palettes off gives the colours back. (Dyl:RibbonSkin "Web") lists the browser modules and assemblies loaded, the browser windows with the classes of their parents, the controls with their page, and the browser windows of AcWebView with their page; with "Detail", it writes to %TEMP%\DynALisp-Web.txt the functions exported by AcWebView.dll (in AutoCAD 2027, the Start tab is a WebView2 made in C++ there, in an MFC window of the MDI client, out of reach of the managed search) and the types and members of AppHomeInterop.
  • FileTabs.cs – an experiment too: a band of colour on the file tab of each drawing of a project, while Display is on and its File tabs is Project or Sheet set in Dyl-Settings (DynalispDisplayOn, DynalispFileTabColors, read from the registry when the plugin loads and again by (Dyl:RibbonFileTabs) and (Dyl:RibbonSkin)). The file tabs have no API: they are a WPF control of AcWindows.dll (Autodesk.AutoCAD.UserControls.DocumentSwitchControl, internal), found among the WPF windows of AutoCAD (PresentationSource.CurrentSources) by the name of its type. Its tabs are the items of tabDrawings (and of MoreControl, the list of the tabs that do not fit), each a WindowData; the tab of a drawing is a DrawingWindowData, whose Doc property is its Document. The project of a drawing is the first folder holding a .apj (Project, as Remote>>Dyl:RevProject) or a .dst (Sheet set, as Dyl-OpenFolder), from the folder of the drawing up, kept per folder until the drawings open change. Each project takes, for the session, the colour of its name in the palette (FNV-1a of the name of its file, in lower case, so the same on every computer), the next one free when another project open has it; a project open keeps its colour. The band is a bar 2 px wide just left of the title of the tab, slanted as its separators (the leftmost leaning edge of the shapes of the tab: in AutoCAD 2027 a tab is a Grid of a Rectangle and Paths 10 px wide, the / between the tabs), from its top to its bottom (down the left side of a tab of the list). It is drawn by an element of DynALisp (Overlay, never hit by the mouse) added over the first grid of the tab that is always shown, so it does not depend on the mouse and the control and its size are not changed; a tab with no such grid gets an adorner (Strip) when it has an adorner layer above it (AutoCAD 2027 has none), else nothing. No element of the template is ever recoloured: the first Border of the tab is its close button, shown only under the mouse. FileTabs.OnIdle, run by Host.OnIdle twice a second at most, puts the bands on the tabs that changed (a drawing opened or saved elsewhere, a tab dragged, the list opened) and removes those of the tabs gone, all of them when it is turned off. (Dyl:RibbonFileTabs) returns what it does (Project (5 tabs, 2 colours), off, not found: ...), reported by Dyl-Diagnostics (Conflicts); (Dyl:RibbonFileTabs "Tree") writes the control, its tabs with their drawing, project, band, side found and title, and its visual tree to %TEMP%\DynALisp-FileTabs.txt, for a version of AutoCAD whose tabs are not found.

The DLL is built with dotnet build Installer\Ribbon\Plugin -c Release, AutoCAD closed (it keeps the DLL open), for .NET 8: AutoCAD 2025 and later. In an older AutoCAD, the tab is the CUIx alone, in English, without the drop-downs.
