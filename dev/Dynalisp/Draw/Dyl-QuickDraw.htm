Quick Draw

The developer docs of Quick Draw: its files and how its code works. What it does and how to use it are in its User Guide page.

The Preset Engine

Quick Draw is a preset tool of Remote\Dyl-Presets.lsp: a preset is a list of strings (id group name ...), the first three columns the same for every tool, then the values of the tool (the columns of *qd:tiles*). Remote\Dyl-QuickDrawPresets.lsp adds its spec to *dyl:presetspecs* at startup (file, header, default . "QD-*", base . "Pst-QuickDraw", cmd . "Pst-QuickDrawCustom", get . Dyl:QuickDrawGet, run . Dyl:QuickDrawRun...), so the command of each custom preset, Pst-QuickDrawCustom<Name>, exists before the App loads (a dynamic child, Dyl:DynList). A default preset has its id (qd-Brick) and a command of the App (Pst-QuickDrawBrick); a custom one gets cu-<Name> the first time it is saved, kept when it is renamed.

How It Works

  1. The hatch scale is the scale of the material, in the units of its pattern: multiplied by 25.4 for an imperial pattern (acad.pat, most pattern files of their own) in a metric drawing (MEASUREMENT = 1), divided by 25.4 for a metric pattern in an imperial drawing (see Hatch Presets).
  2. The layer of the outline layer key is made current (created first with the key properties when the drawing does not have it), then the outlines are made with the Default method of the preset (asked first when the preset has none). The current layer is restored afterwards, also when the command is cancelled.
  3. Only closed polylines, circles, ellipses and splines are kept: an open outline that was drawn is erased (a selected one is skipped) and an alert is shown.
  4. The outlines are put on the layer of the outline layer key.
  5. One hatch is created on all the outlines with -HATCH (Select objects), associative or not and annotative or not as set in the material, using the pattern, scale and angle of the material (the scale multiplied by the drawing scale divided by the one it was set for, when the material follows it) (SOLID uses neither scale nor angle). Since the outlines are hatched together, an outline inside another one stays open (island). The hatch is then put on the layer of the hatch layer key (created if missing).
  6. Outlines and hatch are one undo step. If nothing was made, nothing is hatched.

Drawing A Preset, In The Code

  1. (Dyl:QuickDrawRun id) – reads the preset (Dyl:QuickDrawGet: Dyl:QuickDrawRead, each row fixed by Dyl:QuickDrawFix, a default filled by Dyl:QuickDrawDefault), reads its material joined to the hatch preset of its view (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialGetAs, column 54, *qd:view*: "Cut" or "Surface"), puts its behaviors and border in its material (Dyl:QuickDrawMat, Remote>>Dyl:MaterialBorderPut), and asks its method when it has none (Dyl:QuickDrawAsk, the last one given kept with setenv).
  2. Dyl:QuickDraw – draws the outline: a standard AutoCAD command run by Dyl:QuickDrawDraw (the user finishes it, the entities after a mark collected by Dyl:QuickDrawAfter), or the DynALisp methods: Linear (Dyl:QuickDrawLinear, the band built from its segments with Dyl:QdBand, Dyl:QdMiter, Dyl:QdCorner, Dyl:QdEnd, a live preview with grdraw: Dyl:QdGrBand), Offset (Dyl:QuickDrawOffsetBands, along a picked curve: Dyl:QuickDrawCurveBand), shrinkwrap (Shrinkwrap).
  3. The hatch – Remote>>Dyl:MaterialHatch fills each closed outline with the pattern, the scale of the drawing (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialScale: the units of the pattern, the drawing scale), the origin and the layer key of the material; Dyl:QuickDrawBorderMake puts the border on its key with its overrides.
  4. An assembly – the layers of the preset (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayers, the Assembly column) are placed across the band (Dyl:QuickDrawLayersBand, Dyl:QuickDrawCurveLayers), each layer hatched with its own material, always its Cut (Dyl:QuickDrawLayerHatch), the Border lines of the assembly as the border of the preset (Dyl:QuickDrawAsmBorder) and its Dividing lines on each join (qd:inner), see Assembly Presets.

The vector helpers Dyl:QdAdd, QdSub, QdMul, QdCross, QdDot, QdInters, QdBulge... do the 2D geometry of the bands.

The Windows

  • Dyl:QuickDrawDialog – the Quick Draw window, Res\Dyl-QuickDraw.xaml, a preset window of the windows plugin, shown by Remote>>Dyl:PresetWin. Its spec gives:
    • wtiles – the controls that are not in *qd:tiles*: Has a border (hbr, the inverse of the No border column 28), Has a hatch (hha, the inverse of the No hatch column 53, *qd:nohatch*; the hatch itself is in the columns 42 to 52, *qd:hatch*, see Hatch Settings), the assembly preset of an assembly (mak, column 38: its id, see Assembly Presets) and the texts of the window, read from the columns Dyl:QuickDrawWinExtra adds to each row for the window only: Custom band settings (mbs, column 41, *qd:bandon*), then 55 the layers of its assembly (3 layers), 56 the own ends and corners of the bands (under Custom band settings, shown while it is on, Remote>>Dyl:MaterialBandText), 57 the width of the layers, 58 the preview of an assembly (its bands, Dyl:QuickDrawGalleryBands, used while "mas,mth=Linear|Offset|" holds; else the preview is the hatch of the material, Dyl:QuickDrawWinMatSpec), 59 the description under the preview of an assembly (mld, Dyl:QuickDrawLayersDesc: its total thickness, then each layer and its thickness on a line, with rtos in the units of the drawing), 60 the summary of the border under Has a border (hbi, Dyl:QuickDrawBorderText: its layer key, then each property it overrides), 61 that of the hatch under Has a hatch (hhi, Dyl:QuickDrawHatchText). 55, 57, 58 and 59 are those of its assembly preset (Dyl:QuickDrawAsmShow), and follow mak in the window (pst/follow). Dyl:QuickDrawDlgRowCheck (the rowcheck of the engine) refuses a preset with neither a hatch nor a border (not an assembly, not Replace hatches), and Structural layer on a Linear preset whose assembly has no structural layer.
    • mview – 54: the view of a preset (mvw, a tile of *qd:tiles*, Cut or Surface; Remote>>Dyl:MaterialView, in Dyl:QuickDrawFix, gives a preset of an older file Surface in the Elevation group, and Remote>>Dyl:MaterialMoved the material Brick, Surface, to a preset of the cu-ModularBrick material of DynALisp 1.1 that no longer exists, *mt:moved*). The engine reads the materials joined to their Cut (pst:mats) and to their Surface (pst:smats, Remote>>Dyl:MaterialReadViews) and gives the window both previews of each material (pst/matview), so the preview follows the view picked at once; Dyl:QuickDrawDlgMat and Dyl:QuickDrawWinPrep take the material of a preset for its view (Remote>>Dyl:PresetDlgMatOf).
    • outline – "hbr": the preview (mpv, in the Preview section) is outlined while Has a border is checked: the editor puts O* before its bands (pst/outline), as Dyl:QuickDrawGallerySpec does for the gallery, so both show a preset the same way. blank – "!hha,mth!=Replace": the preview of a preset without hatch is a space (pst/blank), outlined by its border.
    • wprep / wvals – Dyl:QuickDrawWinPrep reads the layer keys and the materials of every group before each showing, Dyl:QuickDrawWinVals gives the lists of the window (methods, border layer keys, lineweights) with their stored values (key/vals), so the rules of the file test the values, not the indexes.
    • wprefix – "4|Polyline=PL-|*=QD-": the text of a new alias, PL- for a preset drawn with a polyline (column 4, the method).
    • What is greyed out is in the window file (dyl:Win.EnabledBy on each row: mth=Linear|Offset| for the band settings, the empty value standing for Ask when drawn), and the checks of the text boxes are its dyl:Win.Check, with the messages of Remote>>Dyl:MaterialDlgCheck; Dyl:QuickDrawDlgCheck checks them again before saving.
    • The open buttons close the window with their code, Dyl:QuickDrawDlgStatus opens their window (3 Band Settings, 5 Assembly Presets, 12 Border Settings, *mt:borderpick*, 13 Hatch Settings, *mt:hatchsetpick*) and gives what was picked to every preset selected; 8 Preset Groups and 9 Materials are run by the engine (Remote>>Dyl:PresetDlgCode). Then the window opens again on the presets being edited.
  • Dyl:QuickDrawBandDialog – the Band Settings window (Res\Dyl-QuickDrawBandSettings.xaml: the hatch, its scale from the width and its origin on the line, the ends and the corners, with their sketches; Band Settings). Its Pick... closes it with *mt:originpick* (4): the values of the window go into the material, Remote>>Dyl:MaterialOriginPick returns the offsets picked (mor, moa, mof) and the window opens again on them. Custom band settings off, or an assembly, Dyl:QuickDrawMat draws with the defaults: the hatch along the line, square ends, mitered corners, no scale by width and no origin on the line. The justification (mjs, column 7, *mt:justify*) is a tile of the Parameters box and is never folded in: an assembly is drawn on it too.
  • Dyl:QuickDrawBorderDialog – the Border Settings window (Res\Dyl-QuickDrawBorderSettings.xaml; Border Settings) on the border columns of the preset shown (from *qd:border*, 17 to 27, in the order of *mt:bordertiles*; shown by Dyl:QuickDrawPropsDialog, their values from Dyl:QuickDrawPropsVals, with sub 0). Dyl:QuickDrawHatchDialog shows the Hatch Settings window (Res\Dyl-QuickDrawHatchSettings.xaml; Hatch Settings) the same way on the hatch columns, 42 to 52. The border tiles stay in *qd:tiles* without a control in the Quick Draw window, so the engine keeps them as they are. Its open buttons close it, 6 the Layer Keys window (Remote>>Dyl:LayerKeyPick), 11 the color window of AutoCAD (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialColorPick, the override then checked), and it opens again on what was picked. On OK the overrides on are checked (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialDlgCheck) and the columns are given to every preset selected. Has a border is greyed out for an assembly (!mas;mth!=Linear|Offset|), its preview never outlined (pst/outline), and Dyl:QuickDrawMat draws an assembly with No border (column 31 of the material): its lines are the borders of its layers.
  • Dyl:QuickDrawAsmDialog – the Assembly Presets window (Res\Dyl-AssemblyPresets.xaml; Assembly Presets), opened by Dyl:QuickDrawDlgStatus on the assembly of the preset shown; the assembly selected on OK goes to the presets selected. The structural layer of an assembly (column 16 of its rows, the seventh field of a Hatch layer, "1") is kept in qd:layers (nth 8, see Dyl:QuickDrawLayersCore, the distance from the line to the left border of the band that Dyl:QuickDrawLayersBand starts from with Structural), with the line of each layer (nth 7, Center or Edge: Hatch aligned to of its band settings, *qd:layerhatch*, column 6) by Dyl:QuickDrawLayersGet; Dyl:QuickDrawLayerPivot puts it after its options (what Dyl:QdEndPt turns an Angled end around) and Dyl:QuickDrawLayerHatch starts its hatch there.
  • Dyl:QuickDrawGallery – the gallery (Quick Draw Gallery).
