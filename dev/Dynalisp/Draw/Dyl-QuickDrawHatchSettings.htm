Hatch Settings

The developer docs of Hatch Settings: its files and how its code works. What it does and how to use it are in its User Guide page.

DataThe hatch columns of the preset in QuickDraw.csv (42 to 52, from *qd:hatch*: HatchKey, then the overrides in the order of *mt:hatchtiles*; No hatch in 53, *qd:nohatch*); for a layer of an assembly, the hatch field of its layer in QuickDrawAssemblies.csv (its key, then ~on~ and the overrides when it has no hatch or overrides one)

The Window

The keys of the window are the hatch tiles of *mt:hatchtiles*: hky the layer key (a list, hky/vals the keys, "" first: From material, the hatch layer key of the material), then each override with the keys of the Border Settings window (bco bcv the color, blo blt the linetype, bso bsc the linetype scale, bwo bwt the lineweight, bto btr the transparency), so the lists of the lineweights are shared. The file is its own, not the Border Settings one, since the session lists of a window are shared with its sub-windows (out/list of the border has no empty item). sub 1 greys out the open buttons okp (Layer Keys, code 6) and bcp (the color window of AutoCAD, code 11).

In the Quick Draw window, Has a hatch is hha (a window tile, the inverse of No hatch, column 53: hha:53:inv in wtiles), greyed out for an assembly and for Replace hatches. Its open button hhe closes the window with *mt:hatchsetpick* (13). Dyl:QuickDrawDlgStatus calls (Dyl:QuickDrawHatchDialog r) on the preset shown, which calls Dyl:QuickDrawPropsDialog with the window file and Dyl:QuickDrawHatchTiles (the hatch tiles with their columns from *qd:hatch*): the same loop as the border, codes 6 and 11 open the Layer Keys window and the color window of AutoCAD, OK checks each override on (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialDlgCheck) and gives the columns to every preset selected; an empty layer key is From material, not refused. The summary under Has a hatch is column 60 of the window (hhi, Dyl:QuickDrawHatchText: From material when the key is empty, since the material can change in the window). The preview of a preset without hatch is a space (pst/blank "!hha,mth!=Replace", see Windows), outlined by its border. Dyl:QuickDrawDlgRowCheck refuses a preset with neither a hatch nor a border (not an assembly, not Replace).

The Assembly Presets window opens the same file itself, on the rows of the layers selected (dyl:Win.Open with dyl:Win.Columns="hky:3,bco:28,...,btr:37"; Has a hatch of a layer, lha, is column 27), with sub 1 and hky/list From preset then the keys (Dyl:QuickDrawAsmWinVals); see Assembly Presets for how a layer keeps its hatch.

When Drawn

Dyl:QuickDrawMat puts the hatch of the preset in the columns of its material with Remote>>Dyl:MaterialHatchOver (its key in column 8 when it has one, each override on in its columns: color 22 and 23, lineweight 26 and 27, transparency 28 and 29, linetype 47 and 48, linetype scale 49 and 50), not for Replace hatches, which gives its material as it is; then No hatch in column 64 (*mt:nohatch*, never saved: the columns of Materials.csv end at 63) with Remote>>Dyl:MaterialNoHatch, never for Replace nor for an assembly. Remote>>Dyl:MaterialHatchAt makes no hatch for a material with No hatch: the outlines are kept as the border (erased only when the preset has no border either, which the window refuses). A Hatch layer of an assembly gets its own hatch the same way in Dyl:QuickDrawLayersGet (the 8th item of its layer, (on overrides...), see Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayers); without hatch it is drawn as a space, its border kept. The gallery and the preview of the Assembly Presets window draw a layer without hatch as a space (Dyl:QuickDrawGalleryLayers, the 11th column of the AssemblyTile).
