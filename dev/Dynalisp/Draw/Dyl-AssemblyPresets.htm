Assembly Presets

An assembly is a preset of the preset engine (Remote\Dyl-Presets.lsp), the tool QuickDrawAssembly of Remote\Dyl-QuickDrawPresets.lsp: (id group name layers), without commands (no cmd: OK returns the assembly selected), its group one of Res\Groups.csv (renamed with the others by Remote>>Dyl:GroupsRename). A Quick Draw preset with Assembly checked keeps the id of its assembly in its column 38 (*qd:layerscol*), which Dyl:QuickDrawMatRaw puts in the column 60 of its material (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialFix keeps a value without : as it is). Dyl:QuickDrawAsmLayers gives the layers of a value: those of the assembly of that id (Dyl:QuickDrawAsmGet, "" when it no longer exists), or the value itself when it holds : (the layers of an older file). Dyl:QuickDrawAsmRead reads the file again only once it changed (vl-file-systime, *qd:asmcache*, cleared when the window saves it).

A Hatch layer is hatched with a material: its ref is the id of that material (Res\Materials.csv), never of a Quick Draw preset. A layer of an older file that names a Quick Draw preset is read as the material of that preset (Dyl:QuickDrawAsmFix, the fix of the assemblies, through Dyl:QuickDrawAsmFixLayer: a ref that is no material but a preset), with the scale and the origin of the hatch of the preset (columns 9, 10, 11, 16 and 12) when both had their own band settings on; Dyl:QuickDrawAsmLayers does the same for the layers of an older preset file. They are written that way the next time the assemblies are saved.

Dyl:QuickDrawAsmMigrate, when the Quick Draw window opens, gives each preset of an older file with its own layers an assembly preset named after it, in its group (one with the same layers used again), and writes both files; a file that cannot be written leaves the presets as they were (their layers still drawn).

How The Layers Are Kept

The Presets And Materials convention keeps a setting that is a list in one column of text, its items divided by | and their fields by :, never ; or , (the separators of the CSV files):

P:mt-Gypsum:16|P:mt-WoodFrame:140|L:THIN:|S::25|P:mt-Brick:90
P:mt-Brick:90:HATCH          ; a Hatch layer with its own hatch layer key
P:mt-Brick:90:~1~1~8~0~CONTINUOUS~0~1~0~25~1~30
                             ; the key of its material, its color and transparency overridden
P:mt-Brick:90:~0~0~7~0~CONTINUOUS~0~1~0~25~0~50::1~A-WALL
                             ; no hatch: the outline only, its own border
P:mt-Brick:90::1~Line~Extended~~45~Lapped~4~First~Interrupted:1~A-WALL
                             ; its own band settings and its own border
P:mt-RigidInsulation:50::1~Line~Square~~45~Mitered~4~First~Interrupted~1~1~0~0~0
                             ; its hatch scaled by its thickness

Each layer is kind:material or layer key:thickness, the kind P (Hatch), S (Space) or L (Line); a Hatch layer can have four more fields: its hatch, its own band settings and its own border, the values of each divided by ~, and 1 for the structural layer. The hatch is its layer key ("": the hatch layer key of its material) alone, or key~on~bco~bcv~blo~blt~bso~bsc~bwo~bwt~bto~btr when it has no hatch (Has a hatch off, 0: drawn as a space, its border kept) or overrides a property (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayerHatchStr; the color written with dots, as the border); Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayers gives it as the 8th item of the layer, on and the 10 overrides. The band settings are on~hatch~ends~extension~angle~corners~miter~lap~joints~msw~mfc~mor~moa~mof (the keys of *mt:bandwindows*, then the scale and the origin of the hatch: Scale by width, width factor, Origin on line, X, Y; hatch Line, Edge or Origin, *qd:layerhatch*; an older file has the first nine, the scale and origin then off, *mt:layerscale*, or a tenth value Edge, read as the hatch on Edge; off 0, kept when they are not the defaults *qd:layerband*, for when they are on again), the border on~key, or on~key~bco~bcv~blo~blt~bso~bsc~bwo~bwt~bto~btr when it overrides a property of its key (the border tiles of *mt:bordertiles*, as the border of a preset; the commas of a color r,g,b written as dots, Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayerBorderStr, and read back as commas; kept off when its key is not the one a new layer gets or a property is overridden). The empty fields at the end are left out, so an older layer reads and writes as before. Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayers reads the text and drops the items that are not valid; Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayersStr writes it; Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayersText gives a summary of them (3 layers).

The Window

  • Dyl:QuickDrawAsmDialog (code 5 of the Quick Draw window, Dyl:QuickDrawDlgStatus) shows Dyl-AssemblyPresets.xaml, a preset window of Remote>>Dyl:PresetDialog, on the assembly of the preset shown (select). It binds the presets of the Quick Draw window (qa:qd) and its preset shown (qa:r): inside the nested preset dialog, pst:lst is the list of the assemblies. On OK the assemblies are saved and the one selected is returned: its id goes to every Quick Draw preset selected.
  • The layers of the assembly shown are a list of rows edited in the window itself (Installer\Windows\Engine\Rows.cs, see Lists Of Rows), held in the column 4 the Lisp adds to each assembly (dyl:Win.RowsIn="4", Dyl:QuickDrawAsmExtra: the rows of Dyl:QuickDrawLayersRows, a row on each line, its columns divided by tabs, Dyl:QuickDrawAsmEncode). The editor loads them each time another assembly is shown and gives each change back to that column; the boxes are greyed out while several assemblies are selected (!pst.many). The rows are (kind material thickness hatch-key line-key band... border... structural): columns 5 to 13 the band settings (on, then the values), 38 to 42 the scale and the origin of the hatch with them (msw mfc mor moa mof), 14 and 15 the border (on, key), 16 Structural, 17 to 26 the properties its border overrides, 27 Has a hatch, 28 to 37 the properties its hatch overrides; the columns a layer does not use come from the new layer (ll/new, qa:def: a Hatch of the first material, 10 thick, a Line on the border layer key of qa:r, the default band settings *qd:layerband*, a hatch on the key of its material), so a layer turned into another kind and back gets its values again.
  • Back from the window, Dyl:QuickDrawAsmWinGet (the wget of the engine) makes the layers of each assembly from its rows (Dyl:QuickDrawLayersOf, then Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayersStr); rows not valid stay in its column 3 as they were edited, and Dyl:QuickDrawAsmCheck (the rowcheck of the engine) says why (also an assembly without a layer that has a thickness): the window opens again on it with its message.
  • The fields are bound to the columns (dyl:Win.Column): lk the kind, lp the material (qa:mats: lp/vals the ids, by group and name, the material of a layer that no longer exists added as (missing material), Dyl:QuickDrawAsmWinPrep), lw the thickness, ly the line layer key, lsr Structural (column 16, dyl:Win.Exclusive: checked on one row, cleared on the others), lbs the band settings on, lha Has a hatch (column 27), lbr Has a border (column 14). The open button of Has a hatch, lho, opens Dyl-QuickDrawHatchSettings.xaml in the window itself (dyl:Win.Columns="hky:3,bco:28,...,btr:37"): the layer key of the hatch in column 3 (hky/list From material, "", then the keys), the properties it overrides in columns 28 to 37; a layer with neither a hatch nor a border is refused. Its open button lbo opens Dyl-QuickDrawBorderSettings.xaml in the window itself (dyl:Win.Open, dyl:Win.Columns="out:15,bco:17,...,btr:26"): the key of the border in column 15, the properties it overrides in columns 17 to 26, its lists given with the values (out/list, out/vals, bwt/list, bwt/vals, Dyl:QuickDrawAsmWinVals) and sub 1, which greys out its open buttons (the Layer Keys and color windows close the window for the Lisp, which a sub-window cannot do). Dyl:QuickDrawLayersOf checks the overrides on (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialDlgCheck). A field changed with several layers selected goes to each one its rules hold for. The assembly has no justification: the line drawn is the Justification of the Quick Draw preset (mjs), whose Structural layer needs a layer checked Structural (Dyl:QuickDrawDlgRowCheck).
  • The open button lbe opens Dyl-QuickDrawBandSettings.xaml in the window itself (dyl:Win.Open with dyl:Win.Columns="mht:6,men:7,...,mjo:13,msw:38,mfc:39,mor:40,moa:41,mof:42"): the columns of the row shown as the values of its keys, its OK gives them to the rows selected that are Hatch layers with their band settings on. Its lists come with the values (Dyl:QuickDrawLayersBandLists: never Ask, Hatch aligned to that of a layer); mth Layer greys out Pick... only (it closes the window for the Lisp). Dyl:QuickDrawLayersOf checks the width factor and the offsets while they are on.
  • The preview is an AssemblyTile (Installer\Windows\Engine\Assembly.cs) that the editor of the rows draws again on each change: lp/specs the preview of each material (Dyl:QuickDrawLayersSpec, as the Gallery), its ppu replaced for a row whose band settings scale its hatch by its thickness (columns 38 and 39, the last two of Columns: the thickness times the factor, in inches of paper), ll/ends the ends of the band, ll/paper the inches of a drawing unit (Dyl:QuickDrawUnitInches: INSUNITS, else MEASUREMENT) and the drawing scale, so the wall and its hatches are drawn at one scale. It has no Line: the line drawn belongs to the Quick Draw preset.
  • Add, Remove, Move up, Move down and Flip run in the window (dyl:Win.Do), New, Copy and Delete of the assemblies too (Engine\Presets.cs), with no call to the Lisp; the text under the list is a dyl:Win.Summary of the rows.
  • In the Quick Draw window, mak (column 38, a wtiles control) lists the assemblies by name (Dyl:QuickDrawWinVals: mak/list, mak/vals the ids, an id no longer found marked missing), and pst/follow 38:42,44,45,46 with pst/follow/rows gives the columns that show an assembly (Dyl:QuickDrawAsmShow: its summary, its width, its preview, its description) those of the one picked, so Width, the preview and its description follow the list at once.

Drawing An Assembly

Dyl:QuickDrawLayersBand (Linear) and Dyl:QuickDrawCurveLayers (Offset) place the layers across the band from the line, by the justification of the preset (Structural: from the middle of the structural layer, Dyl:QuickDrawLayersCore; Center without one, said on the command line); Dyl:QuickDrawLayerHatch hatches each Hatch layer with its material, given the scale and the origin of its own band settings by Dyl:QuickDrawLayerMat (the behavior columns 37 to 40 and 46 of the material; off: its scale, the origin on the line of the layer; the behaviors a material holds for older files are not read), a Space layer is left empty, a Line layer is drawn on its key and replaces the joint it falls on. A Hatch layer whose material was deleted is drawn as a space, with a message.

Each layer of qd:layers (Dyl:QuickDrawLayersGet) carries the options of its own band settings (Dyl:QuickDrawLayerOpts, the list of Dyl:QuickDrawBandOpts, never asked) and its own border, nil when it has none: a list of the border columns of a material (its key at 7, its overrides from 12, Dyl:QuickDrawLayerBorder), drawn by Dyl:QuickDrawBorderKey with Remote>>Dyl:MaterialProps. An assembly has no border of its preset: Dyl:QuickDrawMat sets its No border (column 31), so only the borders with a key are drawn (the layers with their own border, the Line layers). Dyl:QdBandLayers makes a layer with its own options a band of its own: its trims (Dyl:QdTrims) and its ends across its own borders and width. When a layer has its own options or border, the borders are no longer the outline of the whole band and its joins: Dyl:QdLayersBorders outlines each layer from the pieces Dyl:QdBandOne returns (its two sides, its start, its end), a side two layers share (Dyl:QdSamePath) drawn once, by the layer with its own border first, else as the border of the preset (not drawn: an assembly has none) but where a Line layer is; a layer with its own border that owns its four pieces gets its union as one closed polyline. The joints come with the key of their layer, only for a layer whose joints are Interrupted. Along a curve (Dyl:QuickDrawCurveLayers), the curve of a side goes to the key of the own border of the layer before it, else after it, and the lines across the ends are made for each layer.
