Assembly Presets

The developer docs of Assembly Presets: its files and how its code works. What it does and how to use it are in its User Guide page.

Assembly Presets

An assembly is a preset of the preset engine (Remote\Dyl-Presets.lsp), the tool QuickDrawAssembly of Remote\Dyl-QuickDrawPresets.lsp: (id group name layers border-lines dividing-lines exterior units description load-bearing-rating non-load-bearing-rating stc iic family family-description) (see The Assemblies Of The Building Code for units to iic, Families for the last two; exterior First or Last: the end of the layers that is the outside, First for a row of an older file, Dyl:QuickDrawAsmFix), without commands (no cmd: OK returns the assembly selected), its group one of Res\Groups.csv (renamed with the others by Remote>>Dyl:GroupsRename). A Quick Draw preset with Assembly checked keeps the id of its assembly in its column 38 (*qd:layerscol*), which Dyl:QuickDrawMatRaw puts in the column 60 of its material (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialFix keeps a value without : as it is). Dyl:QuickDrawAsmLayers gives the layers of a value: those of the assembly of that id (Dyl:QuickDrawAsmGet, "" when it no longer exists), or the value itself when it holds : (the layers of an older file). Dyl:QuickDrawAsmRead reads the file again only once it changed or in a drawing of other units (vl-file-systime and Dyl:QuickDrawAsmFactor, *qd:asmcache*, cleared when the window saves it).

A Hatch layer is hatched with a material: its ref is the id of that material (Res\Materials.csv), never of a Quick Draw preset. A layer of an older file that names a Quick Draw preset is read as the material of that preset (Dyl:QuickDrawAsmFix, the fix of the assemblies, through Dyl:QuickDrawAsmFixLayer: a ref that is no material but a preset), with the scale and the origin of the hatch of the preset (columns 9, 10, 11, 16 and 12) when both had their own band settings on; Dyl:QuickDrawAsmLayers does the same for the layers of an older preset file. They are written that way the next time the assemblies are saved.

Families

The assemblies are the variants of families: Family (column 13) names the family of the assembly, FamilyDescription (14) its short description, the same for all its variants. BuildAssemblies.py gives each default assembly the family of its number (B6a to B6e: B6, the name without its last lower-case letters, family) and writes its short description from the parts of each row (wall_parts, floor_parts: its framing, its insulation, its channels, its boards, each a list of short labels from the most precise to the most general, layers counting the boards of each face): for each part, the most precise label all the variants share, the parts joined by commas in a few words (family_text: Steel Studs 152, 2-2 Gypsum for S9; 108 families). A row of a file written before the families gets them in Dyl:QuickDrawAsmFix: those of the default assembly of its id (*qd:asmdefs*, read before the file), else its name alone (a family of one variant). Nothing else reads them: Quick Draw draws an assembly by its id, whatever its family.

While DynALisp.Core.dll is loaded, the assemblies are read, fixed and put in the units of the drawing by it (Dyl:AsmDefs and Dyl:AsmRead in Dyl:QuickDrawAsmDefs and Dyl:QuickDrawAsmReadAll, the same rows, checked by the witness files: see Core); a change to Dyl:QuickDrawAsmFix or to the layers and lines of Remote\Dyl-Materials.lsp is made in Installer\Core\Data\Assemblies.cs too, and the witness taken again.

Dyl:QuickDrawAsmMigrate, when the Quick Draw window opens, gives each preset of an older file with its own layers an assembly preset named after it, in its group (one with the same layers used again), and writes both files; a file that cannot be written leaves the presets as they were (their layers still drawn).

The Assemblies Of The Building Code

The default assemblies (as-*, the default AS-* of the spec) are the rows of NBC Tables 9.10.3.1.-A (walls: W, EW, S, B) and 9.10.3.1.-B (floors F1 to F61, the roof R1 and the ceiling membranes M1 and M2, in the groups Floor, Roof and Ceiling), named by their number. They are generated, never edited by hand: Installer\Assemblies\Walls.txt and Floors.txt hold one row of the table per line (its kind of framing, its spacing, its absorptive material, its channels, the boards of each side or of the ceiling, its ratings, STC and IIC as the table writes them, 45 min [1 h(8)]), and python Installer\Assemblies\BuildAssemblies.py writes Res\QuickDrawAssemblies.csv from them: the layers of each one from one face to the other (the materials mt-GypsumTypeX, mt-Gypsum, mt-AbsorptiveMaterial or mt-Void in the cavity, mt-ResilientChannel 13, mt-SteelFurring 22, mt-CMU140 / mt-CMU190, mt-Concrete, mt-HollowCoreSlab, mt-Plywood, mt-GypsumConcrete and mt-SteelDeck for the toppings), the framing of a row of wood studs (mt-WoodFrame~23 at 400 mm or 400/600, ~20 at 600 mm; none for steel and staggered studs), Load-bearing on the studs, blocks, slabs and joists of a load-bearing assembly, the ratings in whole minutes (the first value; a bracketed higher one and the notes in the description), Units mm, Exterior Last for an exterior wall (its sheathing last). The values are those of the 2020 tables, every row (862 assemblies), read from the Quebec Construction Code, Chapter I, Building (NBC 2020 amended), Division B: the English edition gives the descriptions (the French one has slips in some rows), and the ratings were read from both and agree. A row may end with a note of the table (;Notes in Walls.txt), added to its description.

  • Dyl:QuickDrawAsmDefs reads them once from the file as shipped (Remote>>Dyl:PresetDefault, never its user copy, through Dyl:QuickDrawAsmFix, *qd:asmdefs*), the defs of Remote>>Dyl:PresetRead: those missing from a user copy are added when it is read, Reset gives them back (pst/defs), Delete is greyed out for them. wncols 16 makes the window compare the column 15 too (its rows), so a default assembly whose layers only changed is said modified.
  • Units (column 7): "", the units of the drawing (the assemblies the user makes, and those of an older file); mm, millimetres. Dyl:QuickDrawAsmRead gives the assemblies in the units of the drawing (Dyl:QuickDrawAsmToDwg: the thicknesses times Dyl:QuickDrawAsmFactor, 0.001 / Remote>>Dyl:MaterialUnitMetres, 8 decimals), its cache read again in a drawing of other units; everything that draws, previews or adds up an assembly reads it there. The window gets its rows the same way (Dyl:QuickDrawAsmExtra) and Dyl:QuickDrawAsmWinGet writes them back in millimetres (Dyl:QuickDrawAsmFromDwg, 4 decimals: 89 mm shown 3.50393701 in, saved 89 again). Dyl:QuickDrawAsmMigrate reads and writes the file as it is written (Remote>>Dyl:PresetRead), never converted.
  • Description (8, ads), FireRating (9, afl, load-bearing, minutes), FireRatingNLB (10, afn), STC (11, ast) and IIC (12, aii): text boxes under the description (wtiles), checked by the window (empty;integer;min=0) and again by Dyl:QuickDrawAsmCheck (Dyl:QuickDrawAsmRating-p). Dyl:QuickDrawAsmRatingsText (Dyl:QuickDrawAsmMinutes: 60 is 1 h) and the description are added to the description of the assembly in the Quick Draw window (Dyl:QuickDrawAsmShow).
  • The list of the assemblies of the Quick Draw window is sorted with its numbers padded (Dyl:QuickDrawNatKey: W2a before W10a, each name made into its key once, Dyl:QuickDrawAsmSorted, and the list sorted kept with the read it comes from, *qd:asmsorted*); the list of the window itself sorts its families the same way (Natural of Engine\Presets.cs).
  • Dyl:QuickDrawAsmFixLayer reads the materials (to tell a material from the id of an older Quick Draw preset) only for a layer whose id is not mt-*, once for its row: the default assemblies read none.

How The Layers Are Kept

The Presets And Materials convention keeps a setting that is a list in one column of text, its items divided by | and their fields by :, never ; or , (the separators of the CSV files):

P:mt-Gypsum:16|P:mt-WoodFrame:140|P:mt-Void:25|P:mt-Brick:90
P:mt-Brick:90:HATCH          ; a Hatch layer with its own hatch layer key
P:mt-Brick:90:~1~1~8~0~CONTINUOUS~0~1~0~25~1~30
                             ; the key of its material, its color and transparency overridden
P:mt-Brick:90:~0~0~7~0~CONTINUOUS~0~1~0~25~0~50::1~A-WALL
                             ; no hatch: the outline only, its own border
P:mt-Brick:90::1~Line~Extended~~45~Lapped~4~First~Interrupted:1~A-WALL
                             ; its own band settings and its own border
P:mt-RigidInsulation:50::1~Line~Square~~45~Mitered~4~First~Interrupted~1~1~0~0~0
                             ; its hatch scaled by its thickness
P:mt-WoodFiberBatt:140::::1:mt-WoodFrame~23
                             ; load-bearing, a framing of Wood Frame on 23 % of its area
P:mt-ExtrudedPolystyrene:50:::::::1
                             ; the low permeance layer

Each layer is P:material:thickness (an older file also has S::thickness, a Space, and L:key:, a Line, which Dyl:QuickDrawAsmFix turns into a layer of mt-Void and into the lines of the assembly); a layer can have four more fields: its hatch, its own band settings and its own border, the values of each divided by ~, and 1 for a load-bearing layer (the structural layer up to DynALisp 1.1, one at most then); then four fields of what the layer is: its framing id~percent (the material of the framing of a discontinuous layer and its share of the area, more than 0 and less than 100, Remote>>Dyl:MaterialShare-p), and 1 for Variable thickness and Low permeance; every layer without a framing is continuous. The hatch is its layer key ("": the hatch layer key of its material) alone, or key~on~bco~bcv~blo~blt~bso~bsc~bwo~bwt~bto~btr when it has no hatch (Has a hatch off, 0: drawn as a space, its border kept) or overrides a property (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayerHatchStr; the color written with dots, as the border); Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayers gives it as the 8th item of the layer, on and the 10 overrides. The band settings are on~hatch~ends~extension~angle~corners~miter~lap~joints~msw~mfc~mor~moa~mof (the keys of *mt:bandwindows*, then the scale and the origin of the hatch: Scale by width, width factor, Origin on line, X, Y; hatch Line, Edge or Origin, *qd:layerhatch*; an older file has the first nine, the scale and origin then off, *mt:layerscale*, or a tenth value Edge, read as the hatch on Edge; off 0, kept when they are not the defaults *qd:layerband*, for when they are on again), the border on~key, or on~key~bco~bcv~blo~blt~bso~bsc~bwo~bwt~bto~btr when it overrides a property of its key (the border tiles of *mt:bordertiles*, as the border of a preset; the commas of a color r,g,b written as dots, Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayerBorderStr, and read back as commas; kept off when its key is not the one a new layer gets or a property is overridden). The empty fields at the end are left out, so an older layer reads and writes as before. Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayers reads the text and drops the items that are not valid; Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayersStr writes it; Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayersText gives a summary of them (3 layers). Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayers gives a layer as (kind ref thickness hatch-key band border bearing hatch framing variable low-perm), the framing (id percent), the flags "1" or nil.

The Border lines (column 4: the two faces, the ends and the joints of the band) and the Dividing lines (column 5: between two layers) are each on~key or on~key~bco~bcv~blo~blt~bso~bsc~bwo~bwt~bto~btr, as the border of a layer: Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLines reads them (12 values; empty, an older file: off, no key), Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLinesStr writes them. Dyl:QuickDrawAsmFix gives the key of the first Line layer of an older file on a face to the Border lines and that of the first one between two layers to the Dividing lines, both on, when the row has none. Dyl:QuickDrawAsmRow gives the row of the assembly of a preset (or of the layers of an older preset), Dyl:QuickDrawAsmLayers its layers and Dyl:QuickDrawAsmLines its two lines.

The Window

  • Dyl:QuickDrawAsmDialog (code 5 of the Quick Draw window, Dyl:QuickDrawDlgStatus) shows Dyl-AssemblyPresets.xaml, a preset window of Remote>>Dyl:PresetDialog, on the assembly of the preset shown (select). It binds the presets of the Quick Draw window (qa:qd) and its preset shown (qa:r): inside the nested preset dialog, pst:lst is the list of the assemblies. On OK the assemblies are saved and the one selected is returned: its id goes to every Quick Draw preset selected.
  • The list shows the families, called assemblies in the window, which has no word family (pst/noun: 2 assemblies selected), and no count of them above the list (pcn only says No match) ((family . "13,14") of the dialog, pst/family of a preset window): each one once, its first variant by name standing for it, its number of variants beside it (Count of the item). The family box (fam 13, unique among the families; grp; fds 14; aex, abl, adl and the columns of their lines, (famcols . "6,16-39")) gives its values to every variant of the families selected. The variant shown is picked in var at the top of the Layers box (its name, then (famlabel . "9:min,10:NLB:min,11:STC,12:IIC"): its ratings, the non-load-bearing one only when it differs); nam (2, unique among all the assemblies), ads and the ratings are those of the variant shown, or of every variant while several families are selected. vnw (New variant) copies the variant shown, named after the family with the next letter free (NextVariant: B6f, a space before it after a letter), vdl deletes a custom variant of a family that has others. New and Copy add a family of one variant (named after it), Delete deletes every variant of the families selected, all custom. pst.sel is every variant of the families selected; pst.many is on while several families are.
  • The layers of the assembly shown are a list of rows edited in the window itself (Installer\Windows\Engine\Rows.cs, see Lists Of Rows), held in the column 15 the Lisp adds to each assembly (dyl:Win.RowsIn="15", Dyl:QuickDrawAsmExtra: the rows of Dyl:QuickDrawLayersRows, a row on each line, its columns divided by tabs, Dyl:QuickDrawAsmEncode). The editor loads them each time another assembly is shown and gives each change back to that column; the Layers box is greyed out while several families are selected (!pst.many). The rows are (kind material thickness hatch-key line-key band... border... bearing group framing...): the kind always P, column 4 no longer used, columns 5 to 13 the band settings (on, then the values), 38 to 42 the scale and the origin of the hatch with them (msw mfc mor moa mof), 14 and 15 the border (on, key), 16 Load-bearing, 17 to 26 the properties its border overrides, 27 Has a hatch, 28 to 37 the properties its hatch overrides, 43 the group of its material (read, never saved), 44 the material of its framing ("": none), 45 its share in percent, 46 Variable thickness, 47 Low permeance; a new layer is ll/new (qa:def: the first material of the first group, 10 thick, the default band settings *qd:layerband*, a border off on the border layer key of qa:r, a hatch on the key of its material).
  • The column 6 is Exterior (aex, aex/list First layer, Last layer, aex/vals First, Last), the columns 7 to 12 the units, the description and the ratings (see The Assemblies Of The Building Code), the columns 16 to 27 the Border lines split (on, key, the 10 overrides) and 28 to 39 the Dividing lines (Dyl:QuickDrawAsmLinesOf, on the border layer key of qa:r, qa:bk, when they have none; a new assembly has both on). Their checkboxes are controls of the preset (wtiles aex:6,ads:8,afl:9,afn:10,ast:11,aii:12,fam:13,fds:14,abl:16,adl:28); their open buttons abo and ado open Dyl-QuickDrawBorderSettings.xaml on their columns of the assemblies selected (dyl:Win.Columns="out:17,..." on a button of a preset window, Engine\Presets.cs). Dyl:QuickDrawAsmWinGet writes them back to columns 4 and 5, and Dyl:QuickDrawAsmCheck refuses lines on without a key or with an override not valid (Dyl:QuickDrawAsmLinesCheck).
  • Back from the window, Dyl:QuickDrawAsmWinGet (the wget of the engine) makes the layers of each assembly from its rows (Dyl:QuickDrawLayersOf, then Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayersStr); rows not valid stay in its column 3 as they were edited, and Dyl:QuickDrawAsmCheck (the rowcheck of the engine) says why (also an assembly without layers): the window opens again on it with its message.
  • The fields are bound to the columns (dyl:Win.Column): lg the group of the material (column 43, qa:grps: the groups of the materials, Res\MaterialGroups.csv, that have materials, in their order), lp the material (qa:mats: lp/list the names, lp/vals the ids, lp/by the group of each one, by group and name, the material of a layer that no longer exists added as (missing material) without group, Dyl:QuickDrawAsmWinPrep; dyl:Win.ListBy="lg": the list shows the materials of the group of the row, all of them while it has none, and a group changed gives the row the first material of its group), lw the thickness, lsr Load-bearing (column 16, any number of rows), lfm the framing (column 44, lfm/list None then the materials, lfm/vals "" then their ids, every material whatever its group) and lfp its share (column 45, dyl:Win.Check="positive;max=99.9", greyed out without a framing), lvt Variable thickness (46), llp Low permeance (47, dyl:Win.Exclusive="lock": on one row at most, greyed out in the others while one has it, {Binding Free[47]} in the list); the three checkboxes are also in the rows, small boxes (Dyl.Check of Engine\Theme.xaml) under the abbreviations LB, VT and LP, named in a legend under the list (lab), of the list, dyl:Win.Toggle on their column (see Lists Of Rows), lbs the band settings on, lha Has a hatch (column 27), lbr Has a border (column 14). The open button of Has a hatch, lho, opens Dyl-QuickDrawHatchSettings.xaml in the window itself (dyl:Win.Columns="hky:3,bco:28,...,btr:37"): the layer key of the hatch in column 3 (hky/list From material, "", then the keys), the properties it overrides in columns 28 to 37 (a layer with neither a hatch nor a border of its own is a space between the lines of the assembly). Its open button lbo opens Dyl-QuickDrawBorderSettings.xaml in the window itself (dyl:Win.Open, dyl:Win.Columns="out:15,bco:17,...,btr:26"): the key of the border in column 15, the properties it overrides in columns 17 to 26, its lists given with the values (out/list, out/vals, bwt/list, bwt/vals, Dyl:QuickDrawAsmWinVals) and sub 1, which greys out its open buttons (the Layer Keys and color windows close the window for the Lisp, which a sub-window cannot do). Dyl:QuickDrawLayersOf checks the overrides on (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialDlgCheck). A field changed with several layers selected goes to each one its rules hold for. The assembly has no justification: the line drawn is the Justification of the Quick Draw preset (mjs), whose Load-bearing layers needs a layer checked Load-bearing (Dyl:QuickDrawDlgRowCheck). Dyl:QuickDrawLayersOf refuses a framing whose share is not more than 0 and less than 100.
  • The open button lbe opens Dyl-QuickDrawBandSettings.xaml in the window itself (dyl:Win.Open with dyl:Win.Columns="mht:6,men:7,...,mjo:13,msw:38,mfc:39,mor:40,moa:41,mof:42"): the columns of the row shown as the values of its keys, its OK gives them to the rows selected that have their band settings on. Its lists come with the values (Dyl:QuickDrawLayersBandLists: never Ask, Hatch aligned to that of a layer); mth Layer greys out Pick... only (it closes the window for the Lisp). Dyl:QuickDrawLayersOf checks the width factor and the offsets while they are on.
  • The preview is an AssemblyTile (Installer\Windows\Engine\Assembly.cs) that the editor of the rows draws again on each change: lp/specs the preview of each material (Dyl:QuickDrawLayersSpec, as the Gallery), its ppu replaced for a row whose band settings scale its hatch by its thickness (columns 38 and 39, the last two of Columns: the thickness times the factor, in inches of paper), ll/ends the ends of the band, ll/paper the inches of a drawing unit (Dyl:QuickDrawUnitInches: INSUNITS, else MEASUREMENT) and the drawing scale, so the wall and its hatches are drawn at one scale; BorderLines="abl" and DividingLines="adl" draw the lines of the assembly while their checkboxes are on. It has no Line: the line drawn belongs to the Quick Draw preset.
  • Add, Remove, Move up, Move down and Flip run in the window (dyl:Win.Do), New, Copy and Delete of the assemblies too (Engine\Presets.cs), with no call to the Lisp; the text under the list is a dyl:Win.Summary of the rows.
  • In the Quick Draw window, mak (column 38, a wtiles control) lists the assemblies by name (Dyl:QuickDrawWinVals: mak/list, mak/vals the ids, an id no longer found marked missing), and pst/follow 38:42,44,45,46 with pst/follow/rows gives the columns that show an assembly (Dyl:QuickDrawAsmShow: its summary, its width, its preview with its lines, Dyl:QuickDrawGalleryLayers, its description) those of the one picked, so Width, the preview and its description follow the list at once. Those rows are kept (Dyl:QuickDrawAsmShows, *qd:asmshows*) and made again only for another read of the assemblies, other materials or other drawing scale and units: made for the 860 assemblies at each Quick Draw window, they took seconds.

Thermal Resistance And Mass

The data of an assembly are added up from the data of its materials (in SI in Materials.csv), in Remote\Dyl-Materials.lsp:

  • Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayerData – a material per drawing unit of thickness, in the units of the drawing: (r-per-unit r-fixed mass-per-unit), the resistance u / k and the fixed resistance of the material, the mass density × u (u: the metres of a drawing unit, Remote>>Dyl:MaterialUnitMetres, from INSUNITS, else MEASUREMENT); in an imperial drawing (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialImperial-p) multiplied by *mt:asmdata* (R, lb/ft2). nil when not known: no conductivity nor fixed resistance, no density.
  • Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayerValues – one layer: (resistance mass), each nil when not known, from Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayerOf (a material and a thickness); a layer with a framing (a share f) is the parallel path of the isothermal planes method (NBC 9.36.2.4): its resistance 1 / (f / framing + (1 - f) / cavity), its mass f × framing + (1 - f) × cavity, nil when either material has no such value.
  • Remote>>Dyl:MaterialAssemblyData – the layers of an assembly and its exterior side: (resistance mass n-resistance n-mass continuous n-continuous ratio), the totals of the layers whose value is known and how many it is not known for, the resistance of the continuous layers, those without a framing (nil when no layer has a framing: they are then the whole assembly) and how many of them it is not known for, and the low permeance ratio of Remote>>Dyl:MaterialPermRatio (NBC 9.25.5.2: the layers from the exterior side to the one checked Low permeance included, over the others; nil without such a layer, -1 when a layer has no resistance or none is inboard); Remote>>Dyl:MaterialAssemblyText writes them as lines (ASCII: RSI, kg/m2), added by Dyl:QuickDrawLayersDesc to the description of an assembly in the Quick Draw window (the material rows read once by Dyl:QuickDrawWinPrep, qd:mrows).
  • Remote>>Dyl:MaterialDataLists – the same per material, for the Assembly Presets window: Dyl:QuickDrawAsmWinVals gives lp/rsi/list ("per-unit fixed") and lp/mass/list ("per-unit"), aligned with lp/vals, and mt.imp; the window adds them up under the layers as they are edited ({per} and {miss} of dyl:Win.Summary, ltm metric and lti imperial), and shows those of each layer in its row ({Binding P[1:2:lp/rsi:44:45:lfm/rsi:p|–]}, P[1:2:lp/mass:44:45:lfm/mass:a|–]: the framing of column 44 with its share in column 45, from lfm/rsi/list and lfm/mass/list, in parallel for the resistance, by area for the mass), with no call to the Lisp. Under them, {over:...@lfm=@lfm!=} adds up the continuous layers once a layer has a framing (lcm, lcu) and {ratio:first:...@llp} or {ratio:last:...@llp} gives the low permeance ratio (lrf, lrl, shown by the value of aex).

The air films of the faces are not counted, and the thermal bridges only through the framing of a layer: a value is never made up, and they depend on where the assembly is. The framing share is typed (the percentages of the code are in the User Guide); steel framing, which this method does not cover, is not handled.

Drawing An Assembly

Dyl:QuickDrawLayersBand (Linear) and Dyl:QuickDrawCurveLayers (Offset) place the layers across the band from the line, by the justification of the preset (Structural, Load-bearing layers: from the middle of the load-bearing layers together, from the first border of the first one to the far border of the last one, Dyl:QuickDrawLayersCore; the keyword Bearing on the command line, Dyl:QuickDrawAskJus; Center without one, said on the command line); Dyl:QuickDrawLayerHatch hatches each Hatch layer with its material, given the scale and the origin of its own band settings by Dyl:QuickDrawLayerMat (the behavior columns 37 to 40 and 46 of the material; off: its scale, the origin on the line of the layer; the behaviors a material holds for older files are not read); a layer of a material without pattern (mt-Void) is not hatched (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialHatchAt). A layer whose material was deleted is drawn as a space, with a message.

Each layer of qd:layers (Dyl:QuickDrawLayersGet) carries the options of its own band settings (Dyl:QuickDrawLayerOpts, the list of Dyl:QuickDrawBandOpts, never asked) and its own border, nil when it has none: a list of the border columns of a material (its key at 7, its overrides from 12, Dyl:QuickDrawLayerBorder), drawn by Dyl:QuickDrawBorderKey with Remote>>Dyl:MaterialProps. An assembly has no border of its preset: Dyl:QuickDrawMat gives the material its Border lines as its border (Dyl:QuickDrawAsmBorder: their key in column 7, their overrides from 12, No border, column 31, when they are off), the border with no key; its Dividing lines are qd:inner (Dyl:QuickDrawInner, a local of Dyl:QuickDraw: a border list as that of a layer, nil when they are off), the key of each join of Dyl:QuickDrawLayersBand (none when they are off). Dyl:QdBandLayers makes a layer with its own options a band of its own: its trims (Dyl:QdTrims) and its ends across its own borders and width. When a layer has its own options or border, the borders are no longer the outline of the whole band and its joins: Dyl:QdLayersBorders outlines each layer from the pieces Dyl:QdBandOne returns (its two sides, its start, its end), a side two layers share (Dyl:QdSamePath) drawn once, by the layer with its own border first, else as a Dividing line (qd:inner, not drawn when it is nil), the starts, ends and outer sides of the layers without a border of their own as the Border lines (no key), each piece with its key (Dyl:QdOwnAdd); a layer with its own border that owns its four pieces gets its union as one closed polyline. The joints come with the key of their layer, only for a layer whose joints are Interrupted. Along a curve (Dyl:QuickDrawCurveLayers), the curve of a side goes to the key of the own border of the layer before it, else after it, else to the Dividing lines (erased when they are off), the edges as the Border lines, and the lines across the ends are made for each layer.
