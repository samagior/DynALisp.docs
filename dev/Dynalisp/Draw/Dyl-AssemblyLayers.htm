Assembly Layers

The developer docs of Assembly Layers: its files and how its code works. What it does and how to use it are in its User Guide page.

Source FilesModules\Dynalisp\Apps\Draw\Dyl-QuickDraw.lsp (the window, Dyl:QuickDrawLayers*; drawing an assembly), Res\Dyl-AssemblyLayers.xaml (the window, shown by DynALisp.Windows.dll), Remote\Dyl-Materials.lsp (reading and writing the layers)
DataThe Assembly and AssemblyOn columns of Res\QuickDraw.csv

How The Layers Are Kept

The Presets And Materials convention keeps a setting that is a list in one column of text, its items divided by | and their fields by :, never ; or , (the separators of the CSV files):

P:qd-Gypsum:16|P:qd-WoodFrame:140|L:THIN:|S::25|P:qd-Brick:90
P:qd-Brick:90:HATCH          ; a Hatch layer with its own hatch layer key
P:qd-Brick:90::1~Line~Extended~~45~Lapped~4~First~Interrupted:1~A-WALL
                             ; its own band settings and its own border

Each layer is kind:preset or layer key:thickness, the kind P (Hatch), S (Space) or L (Line); a Hatch layer can have three more fields: its own hatch layer key ("": the key of its preset), its own band settings and its own border, the values of each divided by ~. The band settings are on~hatch~ends~extension~angle~corners~miter~lap~joints (the keys of *mt:bandwindows* but the justification; off 0, kept when they are not the defaults *qd:layerband*, for when they are on again), the border on~key (kept off when its key is not the one a new layer gets). The empty fields at the end are left out, so an older layer reads and writes as before. Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayers reads the text and drops the items that are not valid; Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayersStr writes it; Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayersText gives the summary shown beside the open button (3 layers).

The Window

  • Dyl:QuickDrawLayersDialog shows Dyl-AssemblyLayers.xaml (Dyl:WinShow) on the layers of the preset shown; OK gives them to every preset selected in the Quick Draw window, saved with them by Draw or Save.
  • The layers are a list of rows edited in the window itself (Installer\Windows\Engine\Rows.cs, see Windows): ll/rows, a row per layer, (kind preset thickness hatch-key line-key band... border...): columns 5 to 13 the band settings (on, then the values), 14 and 15 the border (on, key). Dyl:QuickDrawLayersRows makes them from the layers, the columns a layer does not use taken from the new layer (ll/new: a Hatch of the first preset, a Line on the border layer key), so a layer turned into another kind and back gets its values again.
  • The fields are bound to the columns (dyl:Win.Column): lk the kind, lp the preset (lp/vals the ids, a preset that no longer exists added as (missing)), lw the thickness (it follows lp/widths, the first width of each preset), lh the hatch layer key (From preset: ""), ly the line layer key, lbs the band settings on, lbr and lbk the border and its key. A field changed with several layers selected goes to each one its rules hold for (Preset and Hatch layer key to the Hatch layers, Thickness to Hatch and Space).
  • The open button lbe opens Dyl-QuickDrawBandSettings.xaml in the window itself (dyl:Win.Open with dyl:Win.Columns="mht:6,men:7,..."): the columns of the row shown as the values of its keys, its OK gives them to the rows selected that are Hatch layers with their band settings on. Its lists come with the rows (Dyl:QuickDrawLayersBandLists: never Ask), the justification of the preset greyed out by mth Layer. A new layer starts from the band settings of the preset (Dyl:QuickDrawLayersBandOf).
  • The preview is an AssemblyTile (Installer\Windows\Engine\Assembly.cs) that the editor of the rows draws again on each change: lp/specs the preview of each preset (Dyl:QuickDrawLayersSpec, as the Gallery; a hatch scale from the thickness gives its ppu as w and the ppu of a unit of thickness), ll/ends the ends of the band, ll/paper the inches of a drawing unit (Dyl:QuickDrawUnitInches: INSUNITS, else MEASUREMENT) and the drawing scale, so the wall and its hatches are drawn at one scale.
  • Add, Remove, Move up, Move down and Flip run in the window (dyl:Win.Do), with no call to the Lisp; the text under the list is a dyl:Win.Summary of the rows.
  • On OK, Dyl:QuickDrawLayersOf keeps of each row what its kind uses and checks it again (the extension, angle and miter limit by Remote>>Dyl:MaterialDlgCheck, a key for a border on); a layer not valid is said (alert) and the window is shown again on the layers edited, the same ones selected (ll.sel).

Drawing An Assembly

Dyl:QuickDrawLayersBand (Linear) and Dyl:QuickDrawCurveLayers (Offset) place the layers across the band from the line, by the justification of the preset; Dyl:QuickDrawLayerHatch hatches each Hatch layer as its preset (its hatch preset, Scale by width, origin), a Space layer is left empty, a Line layer is drawn on its key and replaces the joint it falls on. A Hatch layer whose preset was deleted is drawn as a space, with a message.

Each layer of qd:layers (Dyl:QuickDrawLayersGet) carries the options of its own band settings (Dyl:QuickDrawLayerOpts, the list of Dyl:QuickDrawBandOpts, never asked) and the key of its own border, nil when it has none. Dyl:QdBandLayers makes a layer with its own options a band of its own: its trims (Dyl:QdTrims) and its ends across its own borders and width. When a layer has its own options or border, the borders are no longer the outline of the whole band and its joins: Dyl:QdLayersBorders outlines each layer from the pieces Dyl:QdBandOne returns (its two sides, its start, its end), a side two layers share (Dyl:QdSamePath) drawn once, by the layer with its own border first, else as the border of the preset but where a Line layer is; a layer with its own border that owns its four pieces gets its union as one closed polyline. The joints come with the key of their layer, only for a layer whose joints are Interrupted. Along a curve (Dyl:QuickDrawCurveLayers), the curve of a side goes to the key of the own border of the layer before it, else after it, and the lines across the ends are made for each layer.
