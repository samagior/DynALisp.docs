Assembly Layers

The developer docs of Assembly Layers: its files and how its code works. What it does and how to use it are in its User Guide page.

Source FilesModules\Dynalisp\Apps\Draw\Dyl-QuickDraw.lsp (the window, Dyl:QuickDrawLayers*; drawing an assembly), Res\Dyl-AssemblyLayers.xaml (the window, shown by DynALisp.Windows.dll), Remote\Dyl-Materials.lsp (reading and writing the layers)
DataThe Assembly and AssemblyOn columns of Res\QuickDraw.csv

How The Layers Are Kept

The Presets And Materials convention keeps a setting that is a list in one column of text, its items divided by | and their fields by :, never ; or , (the separators of the CSV files):

P:qd-Gypsum:16|P:qd-WoodFrame:140|L:THIN:|S::25|P:qd-Brick:90
P:qd-Brick:90:HATCH          ; a Hatch layer with its own hatch layer key

Each layer is kind:preset or layer key:thickness, the kind P (Hatch), S (Space) or L (Line); a Hatch layer can have a fourth field, its own hatch layer key. Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayers reads the text and drops the items that are not valid; Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayersStr writes it; Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayersText gives the summary shown beside the open button (3 layers).

The Window

  • Dyl:QuickDrawLayersDialog shows Dyl-AssemblyLayers.xaml (Dyl:WinShow) on the layers of the preset shown; OK gives them to every preset selected in the Quick Draw window, saved with them by Draw or Save.
  • The layers are a list of rows edited in the window itself (Installer\Windows\Engine\Rows.cs, see Windows): ll/rows, a row per layer, (kind preset thickness hatch-key line-key). Dyl:QuickDrawLayersRows makes them from the layers, the columns a layer does not use taken from the new layer (ll/new: a Hatch of the first preset, a Line on the border layer key), so a layer turned into another kind and back gets its values again.
  • The fields are bound to the columns (dyl:Win.Column): lk the kind, lp the preset (lp/vals the ids, a preset that no longer exists added as (missing)), lw the thickness (it follows lp/widths, the first width of each preset), lh the hatch layer key (From preset: ""), ly the line layer key. A field changed with several layers selected goes to each one its rules hold for (Preset and Hatch layer key to the Hatch layers, Thickness to Hatch and Space).
  • Add, Remove, Move up, Move down and Flip run in the window (dyl:Win.Do), with no call to the Lisp; the text under the list is a dyl:Win.Summary of the rows.
  • On OK, Dyl:QuickDrawLayersOf keeps of each row what its kind uses and checks it again; a layer not valid is said (alert) and the window is shown again on the layers edited, the same ones selected (ll.sel).

Drawing An Assembly

Dyl:QuickDrawLayersBand (Linear) and Dyl:QuickDrawCurveLayers (Offset) place the layers across the band from the line, by the justification of the preset; Dyl:QuickDrawLayerHatch hatches each Hatch layer as its preset (its hatch preset, Scale by width, origin), a Space layer is left empty, a Line layer is drawn on its key and replaces the joint it falls on. A Hatch layer whose preset was deleted is drawn as a space, with a message.
