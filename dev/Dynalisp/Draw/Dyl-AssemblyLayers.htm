Assembly Layers

The developer docs of Assembly Layers: its files and how its code works. What it does and how to use it are in its User Guide page.

Source FilesModules\Dynalisp\Apps\Draw\Dyl-QuickDraw.lsp (the window, Dyl:QuickDrawLayers*; drawing an assembly), Remote\Dyl-Materials.lsp (reading and writing the layers)
DataThe Assembly and AssemblyOn columns of Res\QuickDraw.csv

How The Layers Are Kept

The Presets And Materials convention keeps a setting that is a list in one column of text, its items divided by | and their fields by :, never ; or , (the separators of the CSV files):

P:qd-Gypsum:16|P:qd-WoodFrame:140|L:THIN:|S::25|P:qd-Brick:90
P:qd-Brick:90:HATCH          ; a Hatch layer with its own hatch layer key

Each layer is kind:preset or layer key:thickness, the kind P (Hatch), S (Space) or L (Line); a Hatch layer can have a fourth field, its own hatch layer key. Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayers reads the text and drops the items that are not valid; Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayersStr writes it; Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayersText gives the summary shown beside the open button (3 layers).

The Window

  • Dyl:QuickDrawLayersDialog writes its DCL (Dyl:QuickDrawLayersDcl) and edits a copy of the layers; OK gives them to every preset selected in the Quick Draw window, saved with them by Draw or Save.
  • Several layers selected (Dyl:QuickDrawLayersSel): Dyl:QuickDrawLayersEditAll gives a field changed to each layer it applies to (Preset and Hatch layer key to the Hatch layers, Thickness to Hatch and Space); Dyl:QuickDrawLayersEditOne edits the one shown.
  • Dyl:QuickDrawLayersAdd, LayersRemove, LayersMove, LayersFlip; Dyl:QuickDrawLayersPresetWidth gives a Hatch layer the width of its preset; Dyl:QuickDrawLayersTotal the total under the list.

Drawing An Assembly

Dyl:QuickDrawLayersBand (Linear) and Dyl:QuickDrawCurveLayers (Offset) place the layers across the band from the line, by the justification of the preset; Dyl:QuickDrawLayerHatch hatches each Hatch layer as its preset (its hatch preset, Scale by width, origin), a Space layer is left empty, a Line layer is drawn on its key and replaces the joint it falls on. A Hatch layer whose preset was deleted is drawn as a space, with a message.
