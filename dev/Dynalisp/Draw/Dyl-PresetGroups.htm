Preset Groups

The developer docs of Preset Groups: its files and how its code works. What it does and how to use it are in its User Guide page.

Source FilesModules\Dynalisp\Remote\Dyl-Groups.lsp (loaded at startup, before Dyl-Materials.lsp and Dyl-Paths.lsp, whose readers use it), Res\Dyl-PresetGroups.xaml (the window, shown by DynALisp.Windows.dll)
DataRes\Groups.csv (the groups of the presets) and Res\MaterialGroups.csv (the groups of the materials): one group per row, header Group; your copy under User
FollowsThe Presets And Materials convention: a preset is found by the drawing it is used in, a material by what it is made of, each by a list of groups of its own, the same in every window

The Groups

  • Two kinds of groups, in *grp:kinds* ((kind file defaults noun)): nil, the groups of the presets (Groups.csv, *grp:defaults*), and "Material", the groups of the materials (MaterialGroups.csv, *grp:matdefaults*). Every function below takes the kind k; the names without Of are those of the presets.
  • (Remote>>Dyl:GroupsOf k) – the groups of the kind, in their order: its file through Dyl:UserFile, or its default groups when it cannot be read; read again only when the file changed (*grp:cache*, by kind). (Remote>>Dyl:Groups): those of the presets.
  • (Remote>>Dyl:GroupCaseOf g k) – g as written in the groups of the kind, any case (section → Section), else g: a reader of a presets file gives the group of a row through it (Remote>>Dyl:GroupCase for the presets, Remote>>Dyl:MaterialRowFix with "Material"). A group of a presets file that is not one of the groups stays, listed with the others of its window.
  • A preset window asks for the groups with (groups . T) in its dialog spec, or (groups . "Material") for those of the materials (the Materials window; Remote>>Dyl:PresetDlgGroupKind in Remote\Dyl-Presets.lsp). The spec of a presets file in *dyl:presetspecs* says the kind of its groups the same way ((groups . "Material") for Materials.csv), for the renames.
  • (matgroups . T) in the dialog spec (Quick Draw, Quick Shape): the Material list lists the materials of the group picked in a list of its own, mgr (Material group), the groups of the materials and the others of the materials read (Remote>>Dyl:PresetDlgMatGrps). In DCL, pst:mgr is the group picked, set to the group of the material of the preset shown (Remote>>Dyl:PresetDlgMgrShow), and Remote>>Dyl:PresetDlgMatGrp gives the group the Material popup lists; in WPF, the Lisp gives mgr/list and Presets.cs does the same in the window.
  • A default material of an older file in Section or Elevation (groups of the presets, not of the materials) gets its group of *mt:defaults* when it is read (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialRowFix); a material of one's own keeps its group.

The Window

(Remote>>Dyl:GroupsDialogOf n k) opens the Preset Groups window on the groups of the kind k (Remote>>Dyl:GroupsDialog: those of the presets), n the presets of the window it was opened from in each group (code 8 of a preset window). On the groups of the materials, it is titled Material Groups (win/title, read by Session.cs) and counts materials. Its groups are a list of rows edited in the window (Installer\Windows\Engine\Rows.cs): gl/rows, a row per group, (name presets original) (Remote>>Dyl:GroupsRow), the original name "" for a group added; gl/defs the rows of Reset, the default groups (those of the file keep their original name). The name field is unique and checked (dyl:Win.Unique, dyl:Win.Check="text;plain"), the list keeps one group at least (dyl:Win.Check="rows"). The rows whose name differs from their original one are the renames. On OK, the groups are written to the file of their kind (Remote>>Dyl:GroupsWriteOf), and a group renamed is renamed in the files of every preset tool of that kind at once (Remote>>Dyl:GroupsRenameOf: Quick Draw, Quick Shape, the assemblies and the hatch presets; or the materials), and in the presets of the windows open (*grp:renames*, each rename (kind pairs), given by Remote>>Dyl:PresetDlgGroupsSync to the windows of its kind and to their Material group list), even when the window it was opened from is then cancelled.
