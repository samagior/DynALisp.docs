Preset Groups

The developer docs of Preset Groups: its files and how its code works. What it does and how to use it are in its User Guide page.

Source FilesModules\Dynalisp\Remote\Dyl-Groups.lsp (loaded at startup, before Dyl-Materials.lsp and Dyl-Paths.lsp, whose readers use it)
DataRes\Groups.csv (one group per row, header Group; your copy under User)
FollowsThe Presets And Materials convention: the presets and the hatch presets are found by their group, so every window lists the same groups

The Groups

  • (Remote>>Dyl:Groups) – the groups, in their order: Groups.csv through Dyl:UserFile, or the default groups when it cannot be read.
  • (Remote>>Dyl:GroupCase g) – g as written in the groups, any case (section → Section), else g: a reader of a presets file gives the group of a row through it. A group of a presets file that is not one of the groups stays, listed with the others of its window.
  • A preset tool asks for the groups with (groups . T) in its spec (Remote\Dyl-Presets.lsp).

The Window

(Remote>>Dyl:GroupsDialog n) opens the Preset Groups window, n the presets of the window it was opened from in each group. On OK, a group renamed is renamed in the files of every preset tool at once (Remote>>Dyl:GroupsRename: Quick Draw, Quick Shape and the hatch presets), and in the presets of the windows open (*grp:renames*), even when the window it was opened from is then cancelled.
