Band Settings

The developer docs of Band Settings: its files and how its code works. What it does and how to use it are in its User Guide page.

Source FilesModules\Dynalisp\Apps\Draw\Dyl-QuickDraw.lsp, Res\Dyl-QuickDrawBandSettings.xaml (the window, shown by DynALisp.Windows.dll), Remote\Dyl-Materials.lsp (*mt:justify*, *mt:hatchmodes*, *mt:ends*, *mt:corners*, *mt:bandwindows*)
DataThe behavior columns of the preset in QuickDraw.csv (*qd:tiles*); the last answers of the prompts, kept with setenv

The Settings

What the hatch is aligned to, the ends and the corners are behaviors of a preset, never of its hatch preset (Presets And Materials): *mt:justify*, *mt:hatchmodes*, *mt:ends* and *mt:corners* of Remote\Dyl-Materials.lsp list their keys, Ask among them (asked when drawn). A preset draws with them only while Custom band settings is on (BandOn, column 41 of QuickDraw.csv, *qd:bandon*): Dyl:QuickDrawMat folds it into the material it draws with, its ends and corners on (columns 61 and 62 of a material) or, off, the defaults (hatch Line, ends and corners off: square, mitered, joints drawn, Remote>>Dyl:MaterialEndsDefault-p, MaterialCornersDefault-p), the values stored kept for when it is on again; Dyl:QuickDrawMatRaw gives them as stored (the window, Dyl:QuickDrawFix). EndsOn and CornersOn (meo, mcn, 39 and 40) follow it (Dyl:QuickDrawBandFix). A preset of an older file, without BandOn, gets it on when its band was not drawn as the defaults (its own ends or corners, the hatch from the drawing origin, scaled by the width or from an origin on the line), the ends or corners it did not have of its own set to the defaults, so it draws as before. Remote>>Dyl:MaterialBandOwn gives a material with the ends and corners it draws with, and Remote>>Dyl:MaterialBandText the summary under Custom band settings in the Quick Draw window (mei, column 43 of Dyl:QuickDrawWinExtra, the own values of the preset; while it is off the window shows the defaults in its place).

The Window

(Dyl:QuickDrawBandDialog r) shows the Band Settings window for the preset r (the open button mbe of the Quick Draw window closes it with *mt:bandpick*, 3): Dyl-QuickDrawBandSettings.xaml, on the values of Dyl:QuickDrawBandVals for the tiles of *mt:bandwindows* (a list its labels, the keys of its items and the one picked, a box its text), taken from Dyl:QuickDrawMatRaw (the values stored; the button, dyl:Win.EnabledBy="mbs", is greyed out while Custom band settings is off). The method and the justification of the preset are given as mth and mjs, values with no control, for the rules of the sketches: the Justification (*mt:justify*, the line drawn) is a tile of the Parameters box of the Quick Draw window, not of this one (*qd:subtiles* does not have it), and Custom band settings does not fold it in. The window greys out what the choice does not use (dyl:Win.EnabledBy="men=Extended|Ask") and checks the numbers before OK (dyl:Win.Check); the Lisp checks them again (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialDlgCheck). It returns the values, given to the presets selected by Dyl:QuickDrawDlgStatus.

The sketches are drawn in the window file, as those of the docs: one Canvas per item, shown by its rule (dyl:Win.VisibleBy="mco=Lapped,mlt=First"). Inside, the line and its points are a Canvas each for the middle of the band (mjs!=Edge,mht!=Edge,mth!=Offset) and its border (mjs=Edge;mht=Edge;mth=Offset: a layer on Layer edge); the hatch of the next segment is Dyl.Sketch.HatchB along the line and HatchA, as the first one, from the drawing origin (mht); the joint is an outline (mjo=Interrupted), a ghost (mjo!=Interrupted,mht!=Origin) or nothing (see A Sketch Of A Choice).

The same window sets the band of a Hatch layer of an assembly: the Assembly Presets window opens it itself, on the columns of the layers selected (dyl:Win.Columns, see Lists Of Rows), with no call to the Lisp; mth is then Layer, so the scale and the origin of the hatch are greyed out, its lists have no Ask, and Hatch aligned to is that of a layer (*qd:layerhatch*: Line Layer center, Edge Layer edge, Origin).

The Quick Draw window no longer has these values as controls (*qd:subtiles*): they are kept in the columns of the preset and edited here only.

When Drawn

  • (Dyl:QuickDrawBandOpts mat) – the options of a band from the behaviors of its material: an Ask asked now (Dyl:QuickDrawAskKey, the last answer by default), the extension nil for half the width, the end angle in radians.
  • Dyl:QuickDrawAskEnds, Dyl:QuickDrawAskCorners – the Ends and Corners options of the prompts of Linear and Offset, for the next bands (qd:opt).
  • The geometry: Dyl:QdEnd and Dyl:QdEndPt (the ends), Dyl:QdMiter, Dyl:QdTrim and Dyl:QdCorner (the corners), Dyl:QdArc (the rounded ones).
