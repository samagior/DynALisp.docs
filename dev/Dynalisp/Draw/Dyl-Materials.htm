Materials

The developer docs of Materials: its files and how its code works. What it does and how to use it are in its User Guide page.

Source FilesModules\Dynalisp\Remote\Dyl-Materials.lsp (the materials and their hatch presets, the hatch, the windows, loaded at startup), Modules\Dynalisp\Apps\Draw\Dyl-Materials.lsp (the command: (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialDialog nil); its children, the material of the objects: see Assign Material)
DataRes\Materials.csv, Res\HatchPresets.csv and Res\MaterialGroups.csv (the groups of the materials; your copies under User); the window Res\Dyl-Materials.xaml
FollowsThe Presets And Materials convention: material and hatch preset in the windows, the messages, the docs, the code and the files; the data in SI units

Materials File

The materials are saved in Res\Materials.csv (semicolons, one material per row, with a header; the spec Material). Your changes go to its copy under User; the shipped file under Contents gives the default values (status, Reset Selected, Reset All). The columns, in order: Id, Group, Name; Cut, the id of its hatch preset in section ("": no hatch), and Surface, its hatch preset in elevation ("": the same as Cut; not used yet); then its data, in SI units, "" when not known: Conductivity (W/m·K), Density (kg/m³) Resistance, a fixed RSI (m²·K/W) for what its thickness does not give (an air space, a membrane), Permeability, its water vapour permeability δ (ng/(Pa·s·m)), and Permeance, a fixed water vapour permeance (ng/(Pa·s·m²)) for what its thickness does not give (a membrane, a coating). A file saved before the last two columns reads as is: Remote>>Dyl:MaterialRowFix pads every row to 10 columns. Each row is cleaned by Remote>>Dyl:MaterialRowFix (its group as in the groups, a value of its data a number greater than 0, a decimal comma read as a point). An id starting with mt- is a default material, cu- a custom one.

The Material Joined To Its Hatch Preset

A tool never reads the two files itself: Remote>>Dyl:MaterialGet (one id) and Remote>>Dyl:MaterialRead (every material) return each material joined to its hatch preset (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialJoin): the id, group and name of the material, then the columns of its Cut hatch preset (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialHatchPreset), the row of 64 columns the tools drew with when a material held its hatch, so Quick Draw, Quick Shape, the previews and the assemblies read it as before. A preset draws the hatch preset of its view: Remote>>Dyl:MaterialGetAs, Remote>>Dyl:MaterialReadAs and Remote>>Dyl:MaterialJoinAs take it ("Cut" or "Surface"; Remote>>Dyl:MaterialHatchPresetAs: a Surface left empty is the Cut), Remote>>Dyl:MaterialReadViews returns both from one read, (cut surface); the functions without As join the Cut. The view of a preset is its own column (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialView: an older preset of the Elevation group is "Surface"), and a material folded into another one is in *mt:moved* (cu-ModularBrick: mt-Brick, Surface), applied by Remote>>Dyl:MaterialMoved to a preset whose material no longer exists. A material without a hatch preset, or whose hatch preset no longer exists, is joined to *hp:none*: no pattern, no hatch. Remote>>Dyl:MaterialDefs joins the shipped materials to the shipped hatch presets (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialRowDefs, Remote>>Dyl:HatchPresetDefs, each read again when its file changes). Both files are read by Remote>>Dyl:MaterialBoth: (hatch-presets materials).

Files Of DynALisp 1.1 And Older

Up to DynALisp 1.1, Materials.csv held a hatch in each row (the columns of HatchPresets.csv today). Remote>>Dyl:MaterialOld-p tells a user copy of that kind by its header (Pattern in column 3; the answer kept until the file changes, *mt:oldstamp*), and Remote>>Dyl:MaterialMigrate, called by every reader before it reads, splits it: each material is given, in the order of the file,

  1. the hatch preset already given the same hatch in this split (every column after the name equal), or a custom one of that hatch (made by a split whose materials could not be written);
  2. else the hatch preset of its default material (mt-Brick: hp-Brick), not given a hatch yet in this split, which gets the hatch of the file (the user's changes kept);
  3. else a new custom hatch preset named after the material (Remote>>Dyl:PresetIds: cu-Hardboard when hp-Brick was given another hatch).

The material keeps its id, group and name, with no data. The hatch presets are written first, then the materials; when one cannot be written (open in Excel), nothing more is written, the split is used from memory and made again at the next read (told once a session). Until then the spec (head . 4) keeps the old copy from being read as the new format (Remote>>Dyl:PresetFile), and the writers of every preset file split it first (Remote>>Dyl:GroupsRename, Remote>>Dyl:HatchPresetDialog). Dyl-Diagnostics reports a file not split yet (it never splits it), the materials whose hatch preset no longer exists, and a User copy of the HatchPresets.csv of DynALisp 1.0.

The Code

  • The window – Remote>>Dyl:MaterialDialog (the command, and the open button beside Material of the Quick Draw and Quick Shape windows, through Remote>>Dyl:PresetDlgCode 9) splits an old file first (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialMigrate), then shows Res\Dyl-Materials.xaml, a WPF preset window of the spec Material (Remote>>Dyl:PresetDialog), and returns the material selected on OK (its row of the file), nil on Cancel. Its props:
    • The list shows the Cut hatch of each material: (material . 3) makes column 3 the "material" of the preset engine, its list the hatch presets ((mats . Remote>>Dyl:HatchPresetRead), pst/mats), every one of them whatever the group of the material ((matfree . T), pst/matfree), called hatch presets in the messages (matnoun), previewed by Remote>>Dyl:MaterialWinSpec (mspec, the band of Remote>>Dyl:MaterialBand, also used by the Quick Draw Gallery).
    • sur (Surface, column 4) lists Same as Cut ("") then every hatch preset (sur/list, sur/vals of Remote>>Dyl:MaterialWinVals); cnd, den and rsi are the data (columns 5 to 7).
    • The data are shown in the units of the drawing: in an imperial drawing (MEASUREMENT 0, mt.imp "1", which shows the imperial units of the window file) Remote>>Dyl:MaterialWinRow (wrow, each row given to the window) multiplies each value by its factor of *mt:data* (the vapour values in the US perm of ASTM E96: 1 perm·in = 1.45322 ng/(Pa·s·m), 1 perm = 57.2135 ng/(Pa·s·m²)), 4 significant digits (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialSig), and keeps the SI value of each value shown in mt:shown; Remote>>Dyl:MaterialWinGet (wget) turns them back into SI units, a value shown and not changed into its SI value as it was, so opening and closing the window never rounds a value.
    • Remote>>Dyl:MaterialWinCheck (check) refuses a hatch preset that no longer exists and a value of the data that is not a number greater than 0 (empty: not known).
    • The open buttons close the window with their code: 8 the Preset Groups window on the groups of the materials ((groups . "Material"), Res\MaterialGroups.csv: titled Material Groups); 9 (Cut) the Hatch Presets window on the Cut hatch preset ((matdialog . Remote>>Dyl:HatchPresetDialog)), the one selected on OK given to column 3 of the materials selected; 10 (Surface, Remote>>Dyl:MaterialWinStatus, status) the same window on the Surface hatch preset (the Cut one when Same as Cut), given to column 4. The window then opens again.
    • New starts from *mt:newrow*: group Section, no hatch preset, no data.
  • The hatch, its scale and its previews – see Hatch Presets: they are read from the material joined to its hatch preset.
  • The material of the objects – every hatch made carries the id of its material in its extended data, and the child commands give, match and show it: see Assign Material.
