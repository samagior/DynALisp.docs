Materials

The developer docs of Materials: its files and how its code works. What it does and how to use it are in its User Guide page.

Source FilesModules\Dynalisp\Remote\Dyl-Materials.lsp (the materials and their hatch presets, the hatch, the windows, loaded at startup), Modules\Dynalisp\Apps\Draw\Dyl-Materials.lsp (the command: (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialDialog nil); its children, the material of the objects: see Assign Material)
DataRes\Materials.csv, Res\HatchPresets.csv and Res\MaterialGroups.csv (the groups of the materials; your copies under User); the window Res\Dyl-Materials.xaml
FollowsThe Presets And Materials convention: material and hatch preset in the windows, the messages, the docs, the code and the files; the data in SI units

Materials File

The materials are saved in Res\Materials.csv (semicolons, one material per row, with a header; the spec Material). Your changes go to its copy under User; the shipped file under Contents gives the default values (status, Reset Selected, Reset All). The columns, in order: Id, Group, Name; Cut, the id of its hatch preset in section ("": no hatch), and Surface, its hatch preset in elevation ("": the same as Cut; not used yet); then its data, in SI units, "" when not known: Conductivity (W/m·K), Density (kg/m³) Resistance, a fixed RSI (m²·K/W) for what its thickness does not give (an air space, a membrane), Permeability, its water vapour permeability δ (ng/(Pa·s·m)), and Permeance, a fixed water vapour permeance (ng/(Pa·s·m²)) for what its thickness does not give (a membrane, a coating). A file saved before the last two columns reads as is: Remote>>Dyl:MaterialRowFix pads every row to 10 columns. Each row is cleaned by Remote>>Dyl:MaterialRowFix (its group as in the groups, a value of its data a number greater than 0, a decimal comma read as a point). An id starting with mt- is a default material, cu- a custom one.

The Material Joined To Its Hatch Preset

A tool never reads the two files itself: Remote>>Dyl:MaterialGet (one id) and Remote>>Dyl:MaterialRead (every material) return each material joined to its hatch preset (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialJoin): the id, group and name of the material, then the columns of its Cut hatch preset (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialHatchPreset), the row of 64 columns the tools drew with when a material held its hatch, so Quick Draw, Quick Shape, the previews and the assemblies read it as before. A preset draws the hatch preset of its view: Remote>>Dyl:MaterialGetAs, Remote>>Dyl:MaterialReadAs and Remote>>Dyl:MaterialJoinAs take it ("Cut" or "Surface"; Remote>>Dyl:MaterialHatchPresetAs: a Surface left empty is the Cut), Remote>>Dyl:MaterialReadViews returns both from one read, (cut surface); the functions without As join the Cut. The view of a preset is its own column (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialView: an older preset of the Elevation group is "Surface"), and a material folded into another one is in *mt:moved* (cu-ModularBrick: mt-Brick, Surface), applied by Remote>>Dyl:MaterialMoved to a preset whose material no longer exists. A material without a hatch preset, or whose hatch preset no longer exists, is joined to *hp:none*: no pattern, no hatch. Remote>>Dyl:MaterialDefs joins the shipped materials to the shipped hatch presets (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialRowDefs, Remote>>Dyl:HatchPresetDefs, each read again when its file changes). Both files are read by Remote>>Dyl:MaterialBoth: (hatch-presets materials). While DynALisp.Core.dll is loaded, both and their defaults are read and fixed by it (Dyl:MatBoth, Dyl:MatDefs and Dyl:HatchDefs, the same rows, checked by the witness files: see Core); a change to Remote>>Dyl:MaterialFix, Remote>>Dyl:MaterialRowFix or Remote>>Dyl:MaterialBoth is made in Installer\Core\Data\Materials.cs too, and the witness taken again.

Files Of DynALisp 1.1 And Older

Up to DynALisp 1.1, Materials.csv held a hatch in each row (the columns of HatchPresets.csv today). Remote>>Dyl:MaterialOld-p tells a user copy of that kind by its header (Pattern in column 3; the answer kept until the file changes, *mt:oldstamp*), and Remote>>Dyl:MaterialMigrate, called by every reader before it reads, splits it: each material is given, in the order of the file,

  1. the hatch preset already given the same hatch in this split (every column after the name equal), or a custom one of that hatch (made by a split whose materials could not be written);
  2. else the hatch preset of its default material (mt-Brick: hp-Brick), not given a hatch yet in this split, which gets the hatch of the file (the user's changes kept);
  3. else a new custom hatch preset named after the material (Remote>>Dyl:PresetIds: cu-Hardboard when hp-Brick was given another hatch).

The material keeps its id, group and name, with no data. The hatch presets are written first, then the materials; when one cannot be written (open in Excel), nothing more is written, the split is used from memory and made again at the next read (told once a session). Until then the spec (head . 4) keeps the old copy from being read as the new format (Remote>>Dyl:PresetFile), and the writers of every preset file split it first (Remote>>Dyl:GroupsRename, Remote>>Dyl:HatchPresetDialog). Dyl-Diagnostics reports a file not split yet (it never splits it), the materials whose hatch preset no longer exists, and a User copy of the HatchPresets.csv of DynALisp 1.0.

The Code

  • The window – Remote>>Dyl:MaterialDialog (the command, the tab of the Manager, and the open button beside Material of the Quick Draw and Quick Shape windows, through Remote>>Dyl:PresetDlgCode 9) splits an old file first (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialMigrate), then shows Res\Dyl-Materials.xaml with Dyl:MatWindow of the Windows plugin (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialTab), and returns the material selected on OK (its row of the file), nil on Cancel. Its values are made, read back, checked and saved by MatTab of the Core with no Lisp in between; the Lisp gives it the units of the patterns of the hatch presets and runs its open buttons. The window and its values are those of a preset window, as the preset engine made them; its Lisp window code is gone, and what it gave is kept by the witness files (Installer\Core\Tests\Witness\Kept, section mattab), which MatTab is checked against:
    • The list shows the Cut hatch of each material: column 3 is the "material" of the preset window, its list the hatch presets (pst/mats), every one of them whatever the group of the material (pst/matfree), called hatch presets in the messages (pst/matnoun), previewed as Remote>>Dyl:MaterialWinSpec previews them (the band of Remote>>Dyl:MaterialBand, also used by the Quick Draw Gallery and Quick Shape).
    • sur (Surface, column 4) lists Same as Cut ("") then every hatch preset (sur/list, sur/vals); cnd, den, rsi, vpm and vpc are the data (columns 5 to 9).
    • The data are shown in the units of the drawing: in an imperial drawing (MEASUREMENT 0, mt.imp "1", which shows the imperial units of the window file) MatTab.Rows multiplies each value by its factor of *mt:data* (the vapour values in the US perm of ASTM E96: 1 perm·in = 1.45322 ng/(Pa·s·m), 1 perm = 57.2135 ng/(Pa·s·m²)), 4 significant digits (as Remote>>Dyl:MaterialSig), and keeps the SI value of each value shown; MatTab.WinGet turns them back into SI units, a value shown and not changed into its SI value as it was, so opening and closing the window never rounds a value.
    • A hatch preset that no longer exists is refused, and a value of the data that is not a number greater than 0 (empty: not known).
    • The open buttons close the window with their code, run by Remote>>Dyl:MaterialTabCode: 8 the Preset Groups window on the groups of the materials (Res\MaterialGroups.csv: titled Material Groups); 9 (Cut) the Hatch Presets window on the Cut hatch preset (Remote>>Dyl:HatchPresetDialog), the one selected on OK given to column 3 of the materials selected; 10 (Surface) the same window on the Surface hatch preset (the Cut one when Same as Cut), given to column 4. The window then opens again.
    • New starts from MatTab.New: group Generic, no hatch preset, no data.
  • The hatch, its scale and its previews – see Hatch Presets: they are read from the material joined to its hatch preset.
  • The material of the objects – every hatch made carries the id of its material in its extended data, and the child commands give, match and show it: see Assign Material.
