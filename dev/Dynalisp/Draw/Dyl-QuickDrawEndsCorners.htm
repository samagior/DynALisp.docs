Ends and Corners

The developer docs of Ends and Corners: its files and how its code works. What it does and how to use it are in its User Guide page.

Source FilesModules\Dynalisp\Apps\Draw\Dyl-QuickDraw.lsp, Remote\Dyl-Materials.lsp (*mt:ends*, *mt:corners*)
DataThe behavior columns of the preset in QuickDraw.csv (*qd:tiles*); the last answers of the prompts, kept with setenv

The Settings

The ends and the corners are behaviors of a preset, never of its hatch preset (Presets And Materials): *mt:ends* and *mt:corners* of Remote\Dyl-Materials.lsp list their keys, one of them Ask (asked when drawn); Remote>>Dyl:MaterialEndsDefault-p and MaterialCornersDefault-p tell the defaults (square, mitered, joints drawn).

The Windows

(Dyl:QuickDrawBandDialog kind r) shows the Ends (kind ends) or the Corners window for the preset r: its DCL written to a temporary file (Dyl:QuickDrawBandDcl), Dyl:QuickDrawBandState greying out what the choice does not use, Dyl:QuickDrawBandValues checking the numbers (an extension, an angle) before OK. It returns the values, given to the presets selected.

When Drawn

  • (Dyl:QuickDrawBandOpts mat) – the options of a band from the behaviors of its material: an Ask asked now (Dyl:QuickDrawAskKey, the last answer by default), the extension nil for half the width, the end angle in radians.
  • Dyl:QuickDrawAskEnds, Dyl:QuickDrawAskCorners – the Ends and Corners options of the prompts of Linear and Offset, for the next bands (qd:opt).
  • The geometry: Dyl:QdEnd and Dyl:QdEndPt (the ends), Dyl:QdMiter, Dyl:QdTrim and Dyl:QdCorner (the corners), Dyl:QdArc (the rounded ones).
