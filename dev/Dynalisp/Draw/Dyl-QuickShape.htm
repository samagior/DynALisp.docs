Quick Shape

The developer docs of Quick Shape: its files and how its code works. What it does and how to use it are in its User Guide page.

Source FilesModules\Dynalisp\Apps\Draw\Dyl-QuickShape.lsp, Remote\Dyl-QuickShapePresets.lsp (its spec), Remote\Dyl-Presets.lsp (the preset engine), Remote\Dyl-Materials.lsp (the hatch, the border columns), Res\Dyl-QuickShape.xaml (the window, a tab of the Manager)
DataRes\QuickShape.csv (the presets), the library drawings of Res\Shapes (*qs:folder*, the whole folder copied to User once a shape is added, Dyl:UserDirW), Res\Materials.csv

The Libraries

  • Every .dwg of the shapes folder is a library and every block in it a shape (layouts, xrefs and anonymous blocks excepted), named <Library>|<Block> (Shapes|Stud-2x4). The base point of the block is the insertion point; its Block Units scale it to the drawing.
  • Dyl:QuickShapeDb opens a library: the document when it is open in AutoCAD, else an ObjectDBX document (Dyl:QuickShapeRelease releases it). Dyl:QuickShapeLibs, Dyl:QuickShapeBlocks and Dyl:QuickShapeList list them.
  • Dyl:QuickShapeStarter makes Shapes.dwg when the folder has no library, from the outlines of *qs:starter* (closed polylines in inches; an outline inside another is an island).
  • Dyl:QuickShapeAdd (Add shape from selection...) makes a block of the objects selected in a library (Dyl:QuickShapeDefine, its name asked by Dyl:QuickShapeAskName); Dyl:QuickShapeOpen opens a library to edit it (Open library).

Placing A Shape

(Dyl:QuickShapeRun id) reads the preset (Dyl:QuickShapeGet), reads its material joined to the hatch preset of its view (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialGetAs, column 17, *qs:view*, the View list mvw of the window; (mview . 17) makes the engine preview that hatch preset; (matgroups . T) puts the Material group list mgr above it, the groups of the materials, see Preset Groups), gives its border to its material (Dyl:QuickShapeMat), and Dyl:QuickShape brings the block into the drawing when it does not have it, inserts it where the user drags it (point, then rotation), explodes it, fills its closed outlines with Remote>>Dyl:MaterialHatch (islands included) and puts the outlines on the border key, in a loop until Enter or Esc.

The Window

Dyl:QuickShapeDialog is a WPF preset window of the engine ((window . "Dyl-QuickShape.xaml"), Remote>>Dyl:PresetWin), as the one of Quick Draw, and the Quick Shape tab of the Manager: c:Dyl-QuickShape runs (Remote>>Dyl:Manager "QuickShape"), which draws the preset of Draw with Dyl:QuickShapeRun once the window is closed. Its Parameters are the Shape list, the scale, the material and its view, and a Border box (the tiles of *mt:bordertiles* at the border columns of the preset, *qs:border*, its rules in the window file); there are no behaviors and no Has a border: a shape always has its border.

  • Dyl:QuickShapeWinPrep, each time the window is shown, lists the shapes of the libraries (qs:shapes), adds those of the presets not found (qs:refs) and the layer keys of the presets (mt:keys, Remote>>Dyl:MaterialDlgKeys).
  • Dyl:QuickShapeWinVals gives the lists: shp/list (Library: Block, (missing shape) for one not found) and shp/vals, mvw, out ((not defined) for a key the drawing does not have) and bwt.
  • Dyl:QuickShapeDlgCheck checks the presets got back (the scale, a shape of the libraries, the border with Remote>>Dyl:MaterialDlgCheck).
  • Dyl:QuickShapeDlgStatus runs the buttons that close the window: 3 Add shape from selection... (the shape given to the presets selected, the window shown again), 4 Open library (the presets saved, then the library opened), *mt:borderkeypick* the open button of the layer key (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialDlgBorderStatus), *mt:colorpick* that of the color (the color window of AutoCAD, its override checked); 8 (Preset Groups) and 9 (Materials) are those of the engine.
