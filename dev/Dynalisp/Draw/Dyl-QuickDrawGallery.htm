Quick Draw Gallery

The developer docs of Quick Draw Gallery: its files and how its code works. What it does and how to use it are in its User Guide page.

Source FilesModules\Dynalisp\Apps\Draw\Dyl-QuickDraw.lsp (Gallery region), Remote\Dyl-Materials.lsp (the tile images)
Commandc:Pst-QuickDrawGallery, a child of Quick Draw

The Window

  • Dyl:QuickDrawGallery writes its DCL to a temporary file (Dyl:QuickDrawGalleryDcl): *qd:gtiles* tiles a page, *qd:gcols* a row. It returns the preset clicked, drawn by the caller with Dyl:QuickDrawRun.
  • Its locals: qg:all (the presets, ((rec . mat) ...), each material with its behaviors and border: Dyl:QuickDrawMat), qg:grps (the Group popup), qg:grp, qg:lst (the presets shown) and qg:page.
  • The group to open on: *qd:gallerygroup*, set by an item of the ribbon ((setq *qd:gallerygroup* "Section") then the command) and read once, else *qd:gallerylast*, the group shown last in the session (the Ribbon convention on items that are not commands).

A Tile

Dyl:QuickDrawGalleryImage draws each tile: Remote>>Dyl:MaterialPatImage reads the pattern of the material (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialPatDef, from its .pat file), clips its lines to the tile (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialPatClip) and draws them with vector_image, the solid fills with fill_image (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialPatFill), in the colors of the material (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialAci); a frame shows the border. Dyl:QuickDrawGalleryName and GalleryCut fit the name under the tile.
