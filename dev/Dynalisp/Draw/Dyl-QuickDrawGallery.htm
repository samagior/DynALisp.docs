Quick Draw Gallery

The developer docs of Quick Draw Gallery: its files and how its code works. What it does and how to use it are in its User Guide page.

Source FilesModules\Dynalisp\Apps\Draw\Dyl-QuickDraw.lsp (Gallery region), the window Modules\Dynalisp\Res\Dyl-QuickDrawGallery.xaml, its tiles drawn by the Windows plugin (Installer\Windows\Engine\Gallery.cs, Engine\Hatch.cs)
Commandc:Pst-QuickDrawGallery, a child of Quick Draw
PictureIts row of Installer\Windows\Shots\Shots.csv, its sample values Samples\Dyl-QuickDrawGallery.txt (its patterns in Samples\Sample.pat, at a drawing scale of 1:48)

The Window

  1. Dyl:QuickDrawGallery reads the materials once, joined to their Cut and to their Surface (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialReadViews: each preset takes the one of its view, the layers of an assembly their Cut, qg:mats), and the presets (Dyl:QuickDrawRead) into qg:all, ((rec . mat) ...), each material with the behaviors and border of its preset (Dyl:QuickDrawMat). A preset whose material is missing is left out.
  2. The groups (Dyl:QuickDrawGalleryGroups): All, every group of Remote>>Dyl:Groups, then a group typed in the presets file that is not one of them, and Recent when a preset of the gallery was drawn in the session. The group to open on: *qd:gallerygroup*, set by an item of the ribbon ((setq *qd:gallerygroup* "Section") then the command) and read once, else *qd:gallerylast*, the group shown last in the session (the Ribbon convention on items that are not commands), matched by Dyl:QuickDrawGalleryMatch (same name in any case, else a name that starts with the other).
  3. Dyl:WinShow opens Dyl-QuickDrawGallery.xaml with grp (the groups and the one picked) and gal/list, one item a preset: "name<TAB>group<TAB>spec<TAB>rank<TAB>kind<TAB>methods<TAB>default": its rank among the presets drawn last (Dyl:QuickDrawGalleryRank, empty when it is not one of them), its kind (Dyl:QuickDrawGalleryKind: Assembly when Dyl:QuickDrawDlgAssembly-p, else the Type of its material), the methods it can be drawn with, divided by commas (Dyl:QuickDrawGalleryMethods: Linear and Offset for an assembly, else "", Ask when drawn, and every method of *qd:modes*) and its method (column 4). gal/kinds/list gives the kind pills (Drafting, Model, Assembly), gal/methods/list the method pills ("value<TAB>label<TAB>tooltip", the labels the values of *qd:modes*, Ask for ""). The grp list is hidden: the gallery fills the panel pills with a pill for each of its groups that has tiles (Pills, Recent="Recent" of Gallery.cs), a pill picked picks its group, so the Lisp still gets grp back. The pills and the search box filter the tiles in the window itself: nothing calls the Lisp back.
  4. The presets drawn last: Dyl:QuickDrawRun adds the id of each preset it draws to *qd:recent* (Dyl:QuickDrawRecentAdd, the last one first, 12 at most), kept on the blackboard (vl-bb-set) so every drawing of the session shares it; it is not saved.
  5. A tile clicked is selected: gal gets its index, the pills of its methods show, its default method picked (gal.method). A tile double-clicked, or Draw (enabled by gal!=-1), closes the window with 1: the preset of gal in qg:all is returned with the method picked in its column 4 (when it is one of its methods), and drawn by the caller with Dyl:QuickDrawRun; the presets file is not changed. Quick Draw Manager closes it with 2: Dyl:QuickDrawDialog opens in its place. The group shown is kept in *qd:gallerylast*.

A Tile

  • Dyl:QuickDrawGallerySpec gives the spec of the HatchTile of a preset (see The Preview Of A Hatch): one band, its hatch, or for an assembly (column 37 on, its layers read by Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayers) a band per layer, as wide as its thickness, each drawn with the hatch of its own material (Dyl:QuickDrawGalleryMat: among the materials read, qg:mats in the gallery, pst:mats in the Quick Draw window).
  • Dyl:QuickDrawGalleryBand writes a band: F for SOLID, X for a preset missing, else P with the pattern, its .pat file (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialPatUnits, which also gives its units), the inches on paper of one unit of the pattern, its angle and the drawing scale it is divided by: 1 when the material is annotative or adaptive (columns 9 and 10; a Model material has both off, Remote>>Dyl:MaterialFix), else Remote>>Dyl:MaterialDrawScale.
  • The plugin reads the .pat file, draws the pattern 1:1 on paper; only a Model hatch zooms out, with a scale label, when it does not repeat enough in the tile (a Drafting hatch is never zoomed). The colors are those of the theme, not of the material.
