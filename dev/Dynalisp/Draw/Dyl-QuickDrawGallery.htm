Quick Draw Gallery

The developer docs of Quick Draw Gallery: its files and how its code works. What it does and how to use it are in its User Guide page.

Source FilesModules\Dynalisp\Apps\Draw\Dyl-QuickDraw.lsp (Gallery region), the window Modules\Dynalisp\Res\Dyl-QuickDrawGallery.xaml, its tiles drawn by the Windows plugin (Installer\Windows\Engine\Gallery.cs, Engine\Hatch.cs)
Commandc:Pst-QuickDrawGallery, a child of Quick Draw
PictureIts row of Installer\Windows\Shots\Shots.csv, its sample values Samples\Dyl-QuickDrawGallery.txt (its patterns in Samples\Sample.pat, at a drawing scale of 1:48)

The Window

  1. Dyl:QuickDrawGallery reads the hatch presets once (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialRead) and the presets (Dyl:QuickDrawRead) into qg:all, ((rec . mat) ...), each material with the behaviors and border of its preset (Dyl:QuickDrawMat). A preset whose hatch preset is missing is left out.
  2. The groups (Dyl:QuickDrawGalleryGroups): All, every group of Remote>>Dyl:Groups, then a group typed in the presets file that is not one of them. The group to open on: *qd:gallerygroup*, set by an item of the ribbon ((setq *qd:gallerygroup* "Section") then the command) and read once, else *qd:gallerylast*, the group shown last in the session (the Ribbon convention on items that are not commands), matched by Dyl:QuickDrawGalleryMatch (same name in any case, else a name that starts with the other).
  3. Dyl:WinShow opens Dyl-QuickDrawGallery.xaml with grp (the groups and the one picked) and gal/list, one item a preset: "name<TAB>group<TAB>spec". The group and the search box filter the tiles in the window itself: nothing calls the Lisp back.
  4. A tile clicked closes the window with 1 and gal the index of its preset in qg:all: the preset is returned, and drawn by the caller with Dyl:QuickDrawRun. Quick Draw Manager closes it with 2: Dyl:QuickDrawDialog opens in its place. The group shown is kept in *qd:gallerylast*.

A Tile

  • Dyl:QuickDrawGallerySpec gives the spec of the HatchTile of a preset (see The Preview Of A Hatch): one band, its hatch, or for an assembly (column 37 on, its layers read by Remote>>Dyl:MaterialLayers) a band per layer, as wide as its thickness, each drawn with the hatch of its own preset (Dyl:QuickDrawGalleryMat).
  • Dyl:QuickDrawGalleryBand writes a band: F for SOLID, X for a preset missing, else P with the pattern, its .pat file (Remote>>Dyl:MaterialPatUnits, which also gives its units), the inches on paper of one unit of the pattern, its angle and the drawing scale it is divided by: 1 when the material is annotative or adaptive (columns 9 and 10; a Model material has both off, Remote>>Dyl:MaterialFix), else Remote>>Dyl:MaterialDrawScale.
  • The plugin reads the .pat file, draws the pattern 1:1 on paper and zooms out with a scale label when it does not repeat enough in the tile. The colors are those of the theme, not of the material.
