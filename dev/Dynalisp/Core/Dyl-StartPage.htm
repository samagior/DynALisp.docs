Start Page

The developer docs of the Start page: its files and how its code works. What it does and how to use it are in its User Guide page.

Source FilesInstaller\Windows\StartPage.cs (the Start tab, the values, the buttons, Dyl:StartPage and Dyl:StartPageInfo), Installer\Windows\Activity.cs (the active time, the pins, Dyl:Activity), Installer\Windows\Tasks.cs (the tasks of a project), Installer\Windows\Engine\Start.cs (dyl:TemplateList, dyl:PinnedItems, dyl:ActivityGraph, dyl:RecentDrawings, dyl:ProjectTasks, Page.Of), Installer\Startup\SheetSetMgr.cs (the title of a sheet set, compiled in too), Modules\Dynalisp\Res\Dyl-StartPage.xaml (the page), in DynALisp.Windows.dll (.NET 8 and .NET 10, both committed)
DataThe user settings DynalispStartPage and DynalispActivity (Overrides of Dyl-Settings, Dyl:StartPageSet and Dyl:ActivitySet of dynalisp.mnl), read by the plugin itself from FixedProfile\General, where it also keeps DynalispStartPinnedColumns and DynalispStartRecentColumns (the columns picked on the page); User\Start\Activity\<yyyy-MM>.csv (the active time, Date;Seconds;Drawing, a row per day and drawing, UTF-8) and User\Start\Pinned.txt (a path per line); User\Start\Tasks\<name>-<key>.csv (the tasks of a project, Id;Done;Task;Added;DoneOn, UTF-8, the name of its .apj or .dst and a hash of its path), or <folder of the .apj or .dst>\DynALisp\Tasks.csv once kept with the project (read first while it is there, as the Project Files convention says); the Recent File List key of the product key of AutoCAD (then of the profile); %TEMP%\DynALisp Diagnostics\Last startup.txt (the last startup, for the next session); %TEMP%\DynALisp Start\*.png (the pictures of the drawings, by path, date and size); the last %TEMP%\DynALisp Report <date>\Report.txt of Dyl-Diagnostics
FollowsThe Windows convention (a window file without code, its picture drawn by Shots) and the experiments of the Ribbon convention (what is not found is left as it is, and said)

How It Works

  1. Plugin.Initialize of Bridge.cs starts StartPage.Init: a timer on the thread of AutoCAD (every 200 ms, never in a command) that reads DynalispStartPage every second. Dyl:StartPageSet calls (Dyl:StartPage "Apply") so a change saved in Dyl-Settings is read at once, and sets STARTMODE to 1 when it is 0.
  2. The Start tab has no API. StartPage.Find looks in the MDIClient of the main window for the child that holds a browser window (Chrome_WidgetWin_*, CefSharp or WebView2: a drawing holds none), and the child of it the browser is in. Nothing found: the Start tab is left as it is, said by (Dyl:StartPage).
  3. Build opens Res\Dyl-StartPage.xaml with Session.Open (the theme of AutoCAD, in the look of the Interface), takes its content with Page.Of (its resources and font, the size its file gives for its picture dropped) and shows it in an HwndSource made a child of the window of the tab (WS_CHILD), at its whole client area. The browser window under it is hidden; the page is hidden with the tab and destroyed with it. Each tick keeps it the size of the tab and first of its children, and the browser hidden.
  4. Each time the tab is shown, Refresh gives the page its values (the foot ver; actn while Active time is off; the buttons greyed out) and calls View.Fill; ReadRows then reads on another thread the recent drawings (File1, File2... of Recent File List, 48 at most, their date and the picture of their header: the PNG, or the BMP made a PNG, from the address at 0x0D of the DWG), the active time (Activity.Rows, the months written and the seconds not written yet) and the pins (no time on their cards: the graph shows it), the templates (the one of QNEW, then the .dwt of TemplateDwgPath, 12 at most) and the tasks of the projects and sheet sets pinned, given as rec/rows, act/rows, pin/rows, tpl/rows and tsk/rows. The sheet set or project of each drawing (recent or pinned) is found on that thread too (GroupOf: the kind of File tabs of Display, DynalispFileTabColors, else its .dst, else its .apj, from its folder up, as FileTabs.cs finds it), then named and coloured on the thread of AutoCAD (Groups): the colour its file tabs have this session (FileTabs.Colours of the ribbon plugin, by reflection), else the colour of its name in the same palette (FNV-1a), a dot before its name on the tile or card. The name of a sheet set pinned or in the recent list is its title, read before on the thread of AutoCAD by SheetSetMgr.TitleOf (the database open in the Sheet Set Manager, else opened for the moment and closed), kept with the date of its file; its file name when it cannot be read. A theme changed makes the page again.
  5. The engine gives each control of the page that shows rows (IShowsRows) its table key/rows (null while it is read): dyl:PinnedItems (path, name, kind, folder, time (empty), picture, missing; a card clicked scopes the graph named by its Graph, ActivityGraph.ToggleScope, double-clicked opens it, StartUi.ClickOrOpen; every card and tile marks itself while it is the scope, on Scopes), dyl:ActivityGraph (date, seconds, drawing: a grid of the weeks, Sunday first, its squares sized to fill the width of its card (14 to 32 pixels a week), the levels at 1 s, 1 h, 3 h and 6 h in Dyl.Accent, the years with time, the drawings of the day or month picked) and dyl:RecentDrawings (path, name, folder, when, picture, missing; filtered by its Search box, counted in its Count text, its first Max shown; what it can pin given by PinChoices: itself, the first .dst and .apj from its folder up), dyl:TemplateList (path, name, default) and dyl:ProjectTasks (project, name, kind, id, done, text, error: a card per project, its bar, its tasks as check boxes and a box to add one). No control shows a path, only its tip. The page is a grid of three columns as wide as the tab: 240 pixels near its left edge and 320 near its right edge (56 from them), 900 in the middle of the space between them (28 at least on each side); each part is as wide as its column, the cards and the tiles widened to fill their rows (StartUi.FillRow: as many as fit at CardWidth or TileWidth, the recent drawings on Rows rows). A dyl:ColumnChoice over each (pnc, rcc) sets the Columns of its Target (IColumns): that many cards in a row, the pictures sized with them; picked, it is saved at once in DynalispStartPinnedColumns or DynalispStartRecentColumns (unset: as many as fit), a view choice of the page, given back as its value each time.
  6. A task added, checked or deleted calls ProjectTasksFile.Add, Check or Remove: Tasks.csv opened alone (FileShare.None, tried again for 2 s), read again, the task changed by its id, written back, so two people never lose each other's tasks; then the rows are read again. Keep tasks with the project (a checkable item of the right-click menu of a card, ProjectTasks.Shared; the row field 7 says where they are) calls ProjectTasksFile.Share: the tasks of one file added by their id to the other (under its lock), then that file deleted (and the DynALisp folder of the project, left empty).
  7. The pins are kept by Activity.Pin (6 at most); a pinned sheet set opens by _.OPENSHEETSET with FILEDIA 0 in the drawing open (as Reopen sheet sets does), a project in its folder (ACA has no command to open one).
  8. The buttons are run by the plugin: New (DocumentCollection.Add of the template of QNEW, else acad.dwt or acadiso.dwt by MEASUREINIT), Open... and a drawing double-clicked (DocumentCollection.Open, a drawing open made current), a template clicked (DocumentCollection.Add of it), Open sheet set... (a .dst picked, opened as a pin is), Settings (Dyl-Settings sent to the current drawing or the first open, a new drawing made first when none is), Docs through the engine (the home page of the User Guide, index.fr.html in French).
  9. Report a problem shows the panel rpp. ReportGo sends Dyl-Diagnostics to the drawing with *dg:forissue* set: dg:full then makes its report and zip (without User\Start) and gives them to (Dyl:StartPageIssue report zip) instead of asking. Issue opens the new issue page of *dyl:gitrepo* (DynALisp.bundle) with what the user typed, the summary, the problems (15 at most) and the report in a <details> block, cut so the address stays under 7,500 characters, all through dg:anon already; the zip is put on the clipboard (Clipboard.SetFileDropList), since a link cannot attach a file to an issue. With no drawing open, the last report is used.
  10. The Lisp does not run in the Start tab: Dyl:StartupSummary gives the plugin the startup of each drawing ((Dyl:StartPageInfo ms commands aliases warnings errors steps drawing)), kept in memory and in Last startup.txt for the next session.
  11. The previews (Previews.cs): a recent tile or a pin, right-clicked, offers Choose preview picture..., Set preview area... (a drawing found) and Use its own preview (one chosen), the items of StartUi.PreviewItems, raised as Preview (its path, Picture, Area or Own). Every picture of a drawing on the page is of the shape StartUi.PreviewAspect (1.6), shown UniformToFill, so it fills its frame. A picture is an image decoded by WPF (StartUi.Picture, turned by the EXIF orientation of a photo); of another shape, it is framed first in Preview Picture (Res\Dyl-StartPagePreview.xaml), shown by the plugin with frm its file: dyl:PictureFrame (Engine\Frame.cs) shows it whole with a frame of its Aspect, dragged, sized by its corner or the wheel, kept on the picture, and gives back frm/crop (x y width height in its pixels), cut out by Previews.Choose; then made at most 960 pixels on its longer side and a PNG; an area opens the drawing (made current when open, OpenDoc) and sends it (Dyl:StartPagePreviewArea), which asks for two corners (GetPoint, GetCorner, made WCS) and keeps the layout (CTAB, Model while TILEMODE is 1) and the space (Model, Paper at CVPORT 1, else Viewport). Previews.Make then turns the current view to the area (its corners in the DCS of the view, the area widened about its middle to PreviewAspect, the view as wide or as high as the area in the shape of SCREENSIZE), takes Document.CapturePreviewImage (by reflection: it gives a System.Drawing.Bitmap and this DLL has no WinForms), cuts the area out of its middle and gives the view back. Each QSAVE, SAVE or SAVEAS ended (CommandEnded of each document) of a drawing with an area, while the Start page is on, makes it again once AutoCAD is idle, when the drawing is on the layout and space of the area. Kept in User\Start\Previews.csv (Picture;Layout;Space;X1;Y1;X2;Y2;Drawing, the drawing last since its path can hold a ;, read again under its lock before each change) and User\Start\Previews\<key>-<stamp>.png, a new stamp for each picture so the page reads it again; the pictures no row names are deleted. PinRows and Recent take Previews.Of before the picture of the DWG.
  12. Off, the page is destroyed and the browser shown again.
  13. Activity.Init (also from Plugin.Initialize) starts a timer of 15 s: while DynalispActivity and DynalispStartPage are on, the Start tab hidden, the current drawing saved in a folder, the foreground window one of AutoCAD (GetForegroundWindow) and the last input under 3 minutes old (GetLastInputInfo), the time since the last tick (a minute at most) is added to that drawing and day. Flush writes the whole seconds every 2 minutes, at DocumentToBeDestroyed, BeginQuit and Terminate: the file of the month opened alone (FileShare.None, tried again for 2 s), read again, the time added, written back, so two AutoCAD never lose each other's time.

Diagnostics

The Interface section of Dyl-Diagnostics gives DynalispStartPage and STARTMODE, the lines of (Dyl:StartPage) (the setting, what was found, the page, the recent drawings and their key, the last error), a problem when it is on and not shown (the plugin not loaded, STARTMODE 0, the Start tab not found), and while it is on the windows of the MDIClient and the chain of each browser window ((Dyl:StartPage "Tree"), written to %TEMP%\DynALisp Diagnostics\Start page.txt), to adapt Find to another version of AutoCAD. It also gives DynalispActivity and the lines of (Dyl:Activity) (the setting, the timer, the time of this session and its last drawing, the last write, the months kept, the pins, the previews chosen and the areas, the last error), a problem when it is on without its plugin or could not write.

Limits

  • An experiment past what Autodesk supports: a version of AutoCAD that holds its Start tab another way leaves its own page shown.
  • A drawing saved before AutoCAD 2013 may have only a BMP picture, made a PNG; a DXF has none (a sheet is drawn in its place).
  • The page is in English, as every window of DynALisp.
  • Active time counts the time a drawing is in front and used, not the work done in it: reading a drawing counts, a plot running unattended past 3 minutes does not.
