Quick Shape

Function Syntax DYL-QUICKSHAPE (Quick Shape tab of the Manager) + 6 preset commands – see Commands
Requires AutoCAD for Windows (ActiveX / ObjectDBX); the shape libraries of Modules\Dynalisp\Res\Shapes; Dynalisp preset engine, materials and layer keys (loaded at startup)
Licence GNU General Public License v3

Program Description

Place premade shapes, already hatched: studs, bricks, CMU, steel sections... Each preset inserts a shape from a library drawing, again and again until Enter, and fills its outlines with the hatch of its material, on the layers of its keys. The companion of Quick Draw, with the same window.

How To Use

  1. Type the alias of the shape: QS-STUD2X4... (the table below).
  2. Pick its insertion point, then its rotation; place the next one, or press Enter or Esc to stop.
  3. Change or add presets – type Dyl-QuickShape: the Manager opens on its Quick Shape tab, beside the tabs of Quick Draw, the materials and the hatch presets. Pick a preset, its shape and its material, or click New. Your own shapes are blocks of a library drawing (below).

Shape Libraries

The shapes are blocks in library drawings: every .dwg of Modules\Dynalisp\Res\Shapes is a library, and every block in it is a shape. One drawing with many blocks or many drawings with one block each both work, and both can be mixed (e.g. Wood.dwg, Masonry.dwg, Steel.dwg). A shape is shown as Library: Block. When the folder has no library, a starter Shapes.dwg is made with the shapes of the default presets.

  • Base point – the block base point is the insertion point of the shape.
  • Units – the block units (Block unit in the block definition, INSUNITS when it is made) scale the shape to the units of the drawing: a shape drawn in inches is inserted at the right size in a drawing in millimeters. The starter shapes are in inches. The preset Scale multiplies it.
  • Objects – draw the shapes with closed polylines, circles, ellipses or splines for the parts to fill; other objects (lines, arcs, open polylines) are kept as linework, not filled. Draw them on layer 0 with ByLayer properties: they go on the outline layer key when inserted.

The libraries are read without opening them (ObjectDBX), only the shape inserted is copied, and it leaves no block definition in the drawing. The list of shapes of a library is kept until its file changes, so the window stays fast with large libraries. A library open in AutoCAD is read from the open drawing, saved or not.

Making And Editing Shapes

  • Add shape from selection... – closes the window, then: select the objects of the shape, pick its base point and type its name. The shape is added to the library of the shape of the preset (replaced after confirmation when the name exists), the library is saved, and the new shape is given to the preset. The units of the shape are the units of the current drawing.
  • Open library – saves the presets, closes the window and opens the library of the shape of the preset, to edit its blocks (BEDIT, BLOCK, BASE…). Save it: the next insertion uses the edited shape.
  • New library – save any drawing with blocks to the Shapes folder.

Quick Shape Window

The Quick Shape tab of the Manager: the list of the presets on the left, with its filter, its sort and its Group list, as in the Quick Draw window; the preset picked on the right, its preview and its parameters. Several presets can be selected to change them at once.

  • Material group – the group of the materials the Material list shows, by what they are made of (Metal, Wood...; see Material Groups): it shows the group of the material of the preset, and picking another one lists its materials.
  • Material – the material that fills the shape; the square open button just left of it opens the Materials window.
  • View – the hatch preset of the material the shape is filled with: Cut, the shape cut in a section (a stud, a tube), or Surface, its face seen in elevation (the Cut one when the material has no Surface).
  • Shape – picked from the shapes of every library. A shape used by a preset but no longer found stays in the list, marked (missing shape), until another one is picked.
  • Scale – insertion scale, on top of the block units (> 0).
  • Border – the border of the shape: the layer key its closed outlines and linework go on, and the overrides of its Color, Linetype, Linetype scale, Lineweight and Transparency, as in the Border Settings of Quick Draw; the open button left of the layer key opens the Layer Keys window, the one of the color the color window of AutoCAD. A shape is always drawn with its border: there is no Has a border. Saved with the preset (border columns of QuickShape.csv); a preset of an older file gets the border of its material.
  • Draw saves the presets and inserts the selected one, Save saves without inserting, Cancel discards the changes. Another tab of the Manager saves the presets, as Save, and shows that tab. Open Docs opens this page.

Known Limitations

  • A library saved by Add shape from selection (ObjectDBX) has no thumbnail preview until it is saved again in AutoCAD.
  • Block attributes are not prompted for; dynamic block parameters are lost when the shape is exploded.
  • A nested block of a shape whose name exists in the drawing uses the definition of the drawing.
  • QuickShape.csv cannot be saved while it is open in Excel.

Command Names And Aliases

Each program has one main command starting with Dyl-. The short names are its aliases: they run the same command, and can be added, renamed or removed in Dyl-Suite (saved in Resources\Aliases.csv). The rows marked ↳ are child commands of Dyl-QuickShape: they are run by their alias, or picked from Dyl-QuickShape when it lists them (their command is named Pst-*).
