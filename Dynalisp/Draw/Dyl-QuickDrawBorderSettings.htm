Border Settings

Opened by The open button of Has a border, in the Parameters box of the Quick Draw window, and in the Assembly Presets window for a layer of an assembly

Program Description

Choose the layer, color, linetype, lineweight and transparency of the border of a Quick Draw preset, or of a layer of an assembly.

How To Use

  1. In Quick Draw, check Has a border and click the square button beside it.
  2. Pick the layer key, check what to change and give its value.
  3. Click OK. What is not checked follows the layer.
