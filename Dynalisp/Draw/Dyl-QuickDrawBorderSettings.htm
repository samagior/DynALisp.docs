Border Settings

Opened by The open button of Has a border, in the Parameters box of the Quick Draw window, and in the Assembly Presets window for a layer of an assembly
Requires AutoCAD 2025 or later for Windows
Licence GNU General Public License v3

Program Description

Set the border of a Quick Draw preset: the layer key its outlines go on, and the properties of that key the border overrides, its color, linetype, linetype scale and lineweight, and its transparency. Whether the preset has a border at all is Has a border, in the Parameters box of the Quick Draw window; an assembly has none of its own (Has a border is greyed out), its layers have theirs.

The same window sets the border of a layer of an assembly, and the Border lines and Dividing lines of an assembly: opened from the Assembly Presets window, it edits the layers or the assemblies selected. There, the open buttons of the layer key and of the color are greyed out: pick the layer key in its list and type the color.

How To Use

  1. In the Quick Draw window, pick the presets, check Has a border in their Parameters box and click the square open button beside it: the Border Settings window opens on the border of the preset shown.
  2. Pick the layer key, check the properties to override and give their values, then OK. The text under Has a border sums up the border (MED, Color 1, Lineweight 0.35 mm).
  3. For a layer of an assembly, in the Assembly Presets window, select the layers, check Has a border and click the open button beside it.

Border Settings Window

SettingUse
Layer keyThe layer key of the border: the outlines go on its layer. A key used but not defined is marked (not defined). The square open button beside it opens the Layer Keys window on that key, to create or edit keys (saved on its own OK); double-click a key there to use it.
ColorOptional. Color of the border: an AutoCAD color number (1-255) or a true color r,g,b. The square open button beside it opens the color window of AutoCAD.
LinetypeOptional. Linetype of the border, loaded from acad.lin or acadiso.lin when the drawing does not have it (ByLayer and ByBlock are accepted).
Linetype scaleOptional. Linetype scale of the border, greater than 0.
LineweightOptional. Lineweight of the border.
TransparencyOptional. Transparency of the border, 0 to 90.
  • Each optional setting has a checkbox: its value stays greyed out until it is checked. Unchecked, the border has the property of its layer.
  • OK gives the border to every preset selected in the Quick Draw window (saved with them by Draw or Save), or to every layer selected in the Assembly Presets window, once the values are valid (a message under the boxes says what to fix). Cancel discards the changes, Open Docs opens this page.
  • The open buttons close the window for a moment and open it again on what was picked.
