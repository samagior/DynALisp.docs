Assembly Layers

A Quick Draw preset drawn with Linear or Offset can be an assembly: its band is made of several layers side by side, like a wall (see Quick Draw – Assemblies). Check Assembly of layers in the Behaviors box of the Quick Draw window, then click the square open button beside it: the Assembly Layers window edits the layers of the preset shown (of every preset selected, on OK). The text beside the button gives their number, and Width shows their total.

  • Layers – the layers, in order across the band: the first one on the line (or the object), the others toward the side of the band, with Justification on Edge and with Offset (a wall drawn on its inner face: its inner layer first); from its left border to its right one, looking from the start point, on Center. Each line gives its kind, its preset (or the layer key of a line) and its thickness. Under the list: the width of the band, the total of the thicknesses. Several layers can be selected (Ctrl / Shift click) to remove them at once.
  • Kind – Hatch: a band of its thickness hatched as a Quick Draw preset says; Space: a band of its thickness without hatch (an air space, a cavity); Line: a line along the band, where it is in the list (a vapour barrier, a membrane). A line turned into a Hatch or a Space gets a thickness at once: the width of the preset picked, else the thickness it had before, else that of another layer (10 without one); change it in Thickness.
  • Preset – the Quick Draw preset of a Hatch layer, by group and name. It gives the hatch of the layer: its hatch preset, its Scale by width (the thickness of the layer as the width), its Origin on line and Origin X / Y (from the center of the layer when its own Justification is Center, else from its border nearer to the line, into the layer). Its method, width, ends and border are not used. The presets listed are those saved (a new preset gets its id once saved) that are not assemblies themselves.
  • Hatch layer key – of a Hatch layer: From preset, or another layer key for the hatch of this layer only (the preset itself is not changed), shown in brackets after the preset in the list. Saved in the Assembly column as a fourth field of the layer (P:qd-Brick:90:HATCH), only when it is set.
  • Thickness – of a Hatch or Space layer, in drawing units (greater than 0). Picking a preset puts its width in it (the first one when it has several), unless another thickness was typed.
  • Line layer key – the layer key of a Line layer (the outline layer key of the preset for a new one). The line gets the layer of the key, its properties ByLayer. A line between two layers takes the place of the line of their join.
  • Add – adds the layer of the fields after the layers selected (else last), and selects it.
  • Editing a layer – with one layer selected, the fields show it, and a field changed changes it.
  • Editing several layers – select them with Ctrl or Shift click: the fields show the first one, the heading of the box gives their number (Layer (3 selected)), and a field changed is given to each of them it applies to, their other values kept: Kind turns them all into that kind (a Hatch gets the preset shown and keeps its thickness, a line turned into a Hatch or a Space gets one, a Line gets the layer key shown); Preset and Hatch layer key go to the Hatch layers, Thickness to the Hatch and Space layers, Line layer key to the Line layers. A message under the window says how many changed. Move up and Move down are for one layer.
  • Adding a layer of another kind – Add a copy of the one selected, then change its fields.
  • Remove – removes the layers selected.
  • Move up / Move down – the place of the layer selected, toward the left or the right border of the band.
  • Flip – puts the layers the other way, the last one first. At the start point of Linear (or the selection prompt of Offset), Flip does the same for one band only.
  • OK – gives the layers to the presets selected (saved with them by Draw or Save of the Quick Draw window). Cancel discards the changes. Open Docs opens this page.
