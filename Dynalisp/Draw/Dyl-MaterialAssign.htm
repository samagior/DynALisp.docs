Assign Material

Opened by MTASSIGN, a child command of Materials; with it, MTMATCH and MTSHOW
Works with Materials – the materials it gives
Simulation – simulates what it marks

Program Description

Tell DynALisp what a hatch or an outline is made of, when DynALisp did not draw it: a detail received, a hatch of your own. The material stays with it when copied or moved.

How To Use

  1. Type MTASSIGN and select the hatches and closed outlines.
  2. Pick the material, then OK. None takes it off.
  3. MTMATCH copies the material of one object to others.
  4. MTSHOW says what the objects selected are made of.

Good To Know

  • What Quick Draw and Quick Shape draw already has its material.
  • Only hatches and closed outlines take a material.
  • One U undoes it.
