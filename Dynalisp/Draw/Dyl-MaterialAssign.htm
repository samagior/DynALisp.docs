Assign Material

Opened by MTASSIGN, a child command of Materials; with it, MTMATCH and MTSHOW
Requires AutoCAD 2025 or later for Windows
Licence GNU General Public License v3

Program Description

A drawing knows what each hatch is made of. Every hatch that Quick Draw and Quick Shape make carries its material, and with it the tool and the preset that drew it; a layer of an assembly carries its material too, and its place in the assembly. The material stays on the hatch when it is copied, moved, mirrored or stretched, and a change made to the material in the Materials window applies to every hatch already drawn with it. It is what the thermal simulation of a 2D detail will read, to know where each material is.

The Assign Material window gives a material to hatches and closed outlines that DynALisp did not draw: a detail received from someone else, a hatch made with HATCH, a polyline around an area. MTMATCH copies the material of one object onto others, and MTSHOW says what an object is made of.

How To Use

  1. Type MTASSIGN, or pick Material Assign under the arrow of the Materials button in the Draw panel of the ribbon.
  2. Select the hatches and closed outlines (closed polylines, circles, ellipses, closed splines) to give a material. Objects selected before the command are used as they are; an open object is left out, with a message.
  3. In the Assign Material window, pick the material, then OK. None takes the material off.
  4. To give the material of an object to others, type MTMATCH, pick the object, then select the others.
  5. To see what objects are made of, select them and type MTSHOW: for one object, its material, its data and the tool and preset that drew it; for several, how many of each material.

Assign Material Window

SettingUse
MaterialThe material to give to the objects selected: None, then every material by its group, in the order of the groups of the materials (Group - Name). Type the first letters of a group to jump to it. It opens on the material of the first object selected. The square open button beside it opens the Materials window, to make or change a material; the Assign Material window then opens again.
  • Under the list, the number of objects selected.
  • OK gives the material to every object selected, in one step: U takes it back. Cancel changes nothing, Open Docs opens this page.

Good To Know

  • A material given by hand replaces the one the object had, and the object no longer says which tool and preset drew it.
  • Only a hatch or a closed outline has a material: the region of a hatch is inside its boundary (an island stays outside), that of an outline inside the outline. Hatch a region, or close its outline, before giving it a material.
  • A region without hatch keeps its outline, so it is still there for the simulation: a layer of an assembly made of Void (an air space), or drawn without hatch, keeps a closed outline on the MATREGION layer key, a layer that is not plotted. Erase it with the wall it belongs to.
  • A box of a legend (Draw legend in the Materials window) carries its material as a sample, never as a part of the detail.
  • An object on a locked layer is not changed; the message says how many.
  • A material deleted from the Materials window stays on the objects made of it: MTSHOW marks it (no longer exists) and Dyl-Diagnostics reports it.
