Ends and Corners

Opened by The open buttons of Custom ends and Custom corners, in the Behaviors box of the Quick Draw window
Requires AutoCAD for Windows (ActiveX / Visual LISP)
Licence GNU General Public License v3

Program Description

Choose how the bands of a Quick Draw preset drawn with Linear or Offset end and turn their corners: square, open, extended, round or angled ends; mitered, beveled, round or lapped corners, their joints interrupted or not.

How To Use

  1. In the Quick Draw window, pick the preset and check Custom ends or Custom corners in its Behaviors box.
  2. Click the square open button beside it: the Ends or the Corners window opens.
  3. Pick the settings, then OK. Unchecked again, the bands end square with mitered corners; the settings are kept for when it is checked.
  4. While drawing, the Ends and Corners options of the prompts change them for the next bands. The sketches of each setting are in Quick Draw – Ends and Corners.

Ends Window

SettingUse
EndsHow an open band ends: Ask when drawn, Square (the default), Open (no line across the end), Extended, Round or Angled. See Ends and Corners.
ExtensionExtended: the distance past each end, in drawing units; empty: half the width of the band.
AngleAngled: the angle of the cut from square, -80 to 80 degrees (45 by default).

Corners Window

SettingUse
CornersHow two segments meet: Ask when drawn, Mitered (the default), Beveled, Round or Lapped.
Miter limitMitered: a corner whose miter is longer than this times the width is beveled (4 by default, 1 or more).
LapLapped: the segment that runs through each corner, First segment or Next segment; the other one stops against it.
JointsInterrupted, the default, or Uninterrupted.
  • A value is greyed out when the ends or corners picked do not use it: the extension for Extended, the end angle for Angled, the miter limit for Mitered, Lap for Lapped (all of them with Ask when drawn: the one picked when drawn uses them).
  • OK gives the values to every preset selected in the Quick Draw window (saved with them by Draw or Save), once they are valid (a message under the box says what to fix). Cancel discards the changes, Open Docs opens this page.
  • The ends and corners can also be changed for one band when it is drawn, at the first prompt of Linear or Offset (Ends, Corners).
