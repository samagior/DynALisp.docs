Band Settings

Opened by The open button of Custom band settings, in the Parameters box of the Quick Draw window, and in the Assembly Presets window for a layer of an assembly
Requires AutoCAD 2025 or later for Windows
Licence GNU General Public License v3

Program Description

Choose how the bands of a Quick Draw preset end, turn their corners and line up their hatch. A sketch shows each choice.

How To Use

  1. In Quick Draw, check Custom band settings and click the square button beside it.
  2. Pick the settings, then OK.
  3. While drawing, Ends and Corners change them for the next bands.

Band Settings Window

  • Scale by width – the hatch grows with the width of the band.
  • Origin on line – where the pattern starts on the line; Pick... finds it on a sample.
  • Hatch aligned to – the line, or the drawing for a pattern that runs on across the corners.
  • Ends – Square, Open, Extended, Round or Angled.
  • Corners – Mitered, Beveled, Round or Lapped.
  • Joints – a line between segments, or none.

See the sketches in Quick Draw – Ends and Corners.
