Band Settings

Opened by The open button of Custom band settings, in the Behaviors box of the Quick Draw window, and in the Assembly Layers window for a layer of an assembly
Requires AutoCAD for Windows (ActiveX / Visual LISP)
Licence GNU General Public License v3

Program Description

Set how the bands of a Quick Draw preset drawn with Linear or Offset are made: where the line is in the band, what the hatch is aligned to, how the bands end and how they turn their corners. A sketch under the ends and under the corners shows what is picked, and changes with the justification, the hatch, the lap and the joints.

The same window sets the band of a Hatch layer of an assembly, apart from the other layers: opened from the Assembly Layers window, it edits the layers selected, its Justification is greyed out (the line is the line of the whole band), and Ask when drawn is not offered.

How To Use

  1. In the Quick Draw window, pick the preset, check Custom band settings in its Behaviors box and click the square open button beside it: the Band Settings window opens.
  2. Pick the settings, then OK. The text under Custom band settings sums them up (Center, Extended, Lapped). Unchecked again, the bands are drawn with the defaults (Center, the hatch along the line, square ends, mitered corners); the settings are kept for when it is checked.
  3. While drawing, the Ends and Corners options of the prompts (and Justification with Linear) change them for the next bands. The sketches of each setting are in Quick Draw – Ends and Corners.

Band Settings Window

SettingUse
JustificationLinear only: where the line drawn is in the band. Ask when drawn, Center or Edge (the line is a border of the band, the band on the side picked once the line is drawn, as with Offset). A preset of an older version on Left border or Right border is on Edge.
Hatch aligned toLine (each segment hatched along it, the default) or Drawing origin (the angle of the hatch preset and the origin of the drawing, the pattern continuous across the corners).
EndsHow an open band ends: Ask when drawn, Square (the default), Open (no line across the end), Extended, Round or Angled.
ExtensionExtended: the distance past each end, in drawing units; empty: half the width of the band.
AngleAngled: the angle of the cut from square, -80 to 80 degrees (45 by default).
CornersHow two segments meet: Ask when drawn, Mitered (the default), Beveled, Round or Lapped.
Miter limitMitered: a corner whose miter is longer than this times the width is beveled (4 by default, 1 or more).
LapLapped: the segment that runs through each corner, First segment or Next segment; the other one stops against it.
JointsInterrupted, the default, or Uninterrupted.
  • Under the ends and under the corners, a sketch shows what is picked, as in Ends and Corners: the band, its hatch, the line drawn (dashed) and its points. The line is in the middle of the band with Center, on a border with Edge or with Offset. The corners also show the segment that runs through a lapped corner, the hatch of the next segment (turned along it, or the same as the first one with Drawing origin) and the joint: drawn, dashed when Uninterrupted, gone when Uninterrupted from the drawing origin. With Ask when drawn, the sketch says so.
  • A value is greyed out when what is picked does not use it: Justification unless the method of the preset is Linear (or asked when drawn), the extension for Extended, the end angle for Angled, the miter limit for Mitered, Lap for Lapped (all of them with Ask when drawn: the one picked when drawn uses them).
  • OK gives the values to every preset selected in the Quick Draw window (saved with them by Draw or Save), once they are valid (a message under the boxes says what to fix, and OK waits). Cancel discards the changes, Open Docs opens this page.
  • The justification, the ends and the corners can also be changed for one band when it is drawn, at the first prompt of Linear or Offset (Ends, Corners, and Justification with Linear).
