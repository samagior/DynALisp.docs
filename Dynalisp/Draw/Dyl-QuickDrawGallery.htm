Quick Draw Gallery

Function Syntax PST-QUICKDRAWGALLERY (alias QDG)
Requires AutoCAD for Windows (ActiveX / Visual LISP)
Licence GNU General Public License v3

Program Description

Pick a Quick Draw preset by its look: a tile for each preset, its hatch drawn in it, by group. Click a tile and draw the preset, as its command would.

How To Use

  1. Click Quick Draw on the DynALisp tab of the ribbon (or type QDG). Its arrow lists All and the groups, to open on one group; the button then opens the group picked last.
  2. Pick a group in the window, turn the pages, then click a tile: the window closes and the preset draws.
  3. The presets are edited in the Quick Draw window (Quick Draw Manager beside it on the ribbon).

Quick Draw Gallery Window

  • Group – All, or one of the Preset Groups, in their order, also a group without a preset yet (its page is empty), with the number of presets shown beside it. The window opens on the group picked on the ribbon, else on the group shown last.
  • Tiles – 24 a page, in the order of the presets file: the hatch of the preset and its name under it. Click one to draw it: the window closes and Quick Draw starts with that preset (its method, its behaviors and its border).
  • < Previous / Next > – the other pages of a group of more than 24 presets.
  • Quick Draw Manager... – closes the gallery and opens the Quick Draw window, to edit the presets or create one. Cancel closes the gallery, Open Docs opens this page.

The Hatch Of A Tile

  • Each tile draws the pattern of the hatch preset of the preset, read from the .pat file that defines it (acad.pat or acadiso.pat, else its own file on the support path), turned by its angle, on its background color (white without one). Default and custom presets are drawn the same way, so a custom preset shows what it draws.
  • The scale of the tile is not the scale of the hatch: every pattern is drawn about as dense, so a fine stipple and a large brick both read at a glance. Two presets with the same pattern look the same (Brick and Iron are both ANSI31).
  • An assembly (a Linear preset with Assembly of layers checked) shows its layers side by side, from left to right as in the Assembly Layers window: each band as wide as its thickness (a thin layer still a few pixels wide) and drawn with the hatch of its own preset, a space left white, a line in the color of its layer key.
  • A SOLID hatch is a grey tile. A pattern that no .pat file defines is crossed out in red: its hatch preset needs a pattern file (see Dyl-RebuildPat).
  • The patterns are read once per AutoCAD session: a .pat file edited meanwhile shows its new lines after AutoCAD restarts.

Known Limitations

  • The groups under the arrow of the ribbon button are the default groups: a group renamed or added in Preset Groups is not in that list, but it is in the Group list of the window. A group picked on the ribbon opens on the group of the nearest name (Floor → Floors), else, when it was renamed or deleted, on All.
  • A long name is cut on two lines of about 17 characters.
