Quick Draw Gallery

Function Syntax PST-QUICKDRAWGALLERY (alias QDG)
Requires AutoCAD 2015 or later for Windows, with the DynALisp windows plugin
Licence GNU General Public License v3

Program Description

Pick a Quick Draw preset by its look: a tile for each preset, its hatch drawn as it plots. Filter the tiles by group, by kind or by a few letters of their name, pick one and the method to draw it with, and draw the preset, as its command would.

How To Use

  1. Click Quick Draw on the DynALisp tab of the ribbon (or type QDG). Its arrow lists All and the groups, to open on one group; the button then opens the group picked last.
  2. Click the pill of a group or of a kind, or type a few letters in the search box.
  3. Click a tile to select it: the pills of its methods show under the tiles, its default method picked. Pick another method if you like.
  4. Double-click the tile (or click Draw): the window closes and the preset draws with the method picked.
  5. The presets are edited in the Quick Draw window (Quick Draw Manager beside it on the ribbon, or at the bottom of the gallery).

Quick Draw Gallery Window

  • Group pills – under the search box: All, then a pill for each of the Preset Groups that has presets, in their order, and Recent last. Click one to show only its presets; the one shown is filled in blue. The window opens on the group picked on the ribbon, else on the group shown last.
  • Kind pills – under the group pills: All, Drafting and Model (the type of the hatch preset: a size on paper, or a real size in the model) and Assembly (a preset with Assembly of layers checked). They filter the tiles with the group pills.
  • Recent – the presets drawn since AutoCAD was started, the last one first (twelve at most), whichever way they were drawn (the gallery, an alias, the Quick Draw window). The pill shows once a preset was drawn.
  • Search presets – the box with the magnifier: shows only the presets whose name or group holds the text typed, in any case. It searches the group picked: pick All to search every preset. The number of presets shown is at the right.
  • Tiles – six a row, in the order of the presets file: the hatch of the preset, its name and its group under it. Scroll down for the others. Click one to select it (its border turns blue); double-click it to draw it: the window closes and Quick Draw starts with that preset (the method picked, its behaviors and its border). A long name is cut; its tooltip shows it whole.
  • Method – under the tiles, the pills of the methods the preset selected can be drawn with, its Default method picked (set in the Quick Draw window): Ask (asked on the command line), Polyline, Rectangle, Circle, Polygon, Ellipse, Shrinkwrap, Pick (internal points), Select (closed objects), Linear, Offset and Replace (hatches); an assembly only Linear and Offset, since its layers are bands. The method picked is for this time only: the preset keeps its default method. See Quick Draw – Default Method.
  • Draw – (or Enter) draws the preset selected, as a double-click; greyed out until a tile is selected.
  • Quick Draw Manager – closes the gallery and opens the Quick Draw window, to edit the presets or create one. Cancel closes the gallery, Open Docs opens this page.

The Hatch Of A Tile

  • Each tile draws the pattern of the hatch preset of the preset as it plots: at its size on paper, 1:1 on the screen, read from the .pat file that defines it (acad.pat or acadiso.pat, else its own file on the support path), turned by its angle. Default and custom presets are drawn the same way, so a tile shows what the preset draws.
  • A Drafting hatch preset that is Annotative or has Adaptive scaling keeps its size on paper: its tile is the same at every scale. Any other hatch (a Model hatch preset, a brick in elevation) is drawn at its scale divided by the scale of the drawing (the annotation scale of the current space), so it looks smaller at 1:100 than at 1:50, as on the sheet.
  • When fewer than about three repeats of a pattern fit in the tile (one brick, a large tile), the tile zooms out just enough to show them and gives the scale it uses in its bottom right corner (1:100). A pattern too fine for the screen is drawn grey, as it reads on paper.
  • An assembly (a Linear preset with Assembly of layers checked) shows its layers side by side, from left to right as in the Assembly Layers window: each band as wide as its thickness (a thin layer still a few pixels wide) and drawn with the hatch of its own preset, a space left empty, a line between two bands.
  • A SOLID hatch is a grey tile. A pattern that no .pat file defines, or a layer whose preset is missing, is crossed out: its hatch preset needs a pattern file (see Dyl-RebuildPat).
  • The tiles are drawn in the colors of the theme of AutoCAD (light or dark), not in the colors of the hatch presets.

Known Limitations

  • The groups under the arrow of the ribbon button are the default groups: a group renamed or added in Preset Groups is not in that list, but it has its pill in the window. A group picked on the ribbon opens on the group of the nearest name (Floor → Floors), else, when it was renamed or deleted, on All.
  • The window needs the DynALisp windows plugin: when it is not loaded, the gallery does not open.
