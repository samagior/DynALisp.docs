Shrinkwrap

Function Syntax DYL-SHRINKWRAP
Source File Modules\Dynalisp\Apps\Draw\Dyl-Shrinkwrap.lsp (command) and Modules\Dynalisp\Remote\Dyl-Shrinkwrap.lsp (outline, loaded at startup)
Requires AutoCAD for Windows (ActiveX / Visual LISP)
Licence GNU General Public License v3

Program Description

Draws closed polylines around the outside of the selected objects, like the AutoCAD Architecture AECLINEWORKSHRINKWRAP command, in any AutoCAD. One outline is created for each group of touching or overlapping objects, on the current layer. The selected objects are not changed, and the new outlines are left selected.

Unlike the BOUNDARY command alone, texts are wrapped too: texts, mtexts, block attributes and the text inside blocks, dimensions, multileaders and tables.

Gap Tolerance

Objects that do not close an area (open lines, a drawing with small gaps) are handled with a gap tolerance. DYL-SHRINKWRAP first asks it, 0 by default (exact): with a value, the objects are thickened to bands of that width before the outline is traced, so objects closer than the gap are joined and an open drawing is closed, then the outline is offset back inwards by half the gap: it follows the objects within about half the gap. Straight pieces (lines, polylines without arcs) keep their vertices; arcs, circles, splines and curved polylines are divided every half gap.

When no outline is found (or a try fails), Shrinkwrap tries again by itself with a larger gap: the gap typed plus one gap step, then two, up to 10 steps, and the command line gives the gap that was used (Shrinkwrap: no outline with a gap of 0, found with a gap tolerance of 3 (3 step(s) of 1...)). The gap step is set in Dyl-Settings, Drawing Settings (its open button): one for the imperial drawings (1 by default, inches) and one for the metric drawings (25 by default, mm). A larger gap closes larger openings but rounds the outline more.

In Quick Draw, the gap is a behavior of the material: with Gap override off, Shrinkwrap starts at 0 and uses the gap step of Dyl-Settings; on, it starts with the Gap of the preset (one value, in drawing units), and asks the gap at every Shrinkwrap when that box is empty. The automatic tries apply in every case.

How It Works

  1. Each selected object is added to a boundary set, or replaced by temporary objects that BOUNDARY can trace:
    • texts, mtexts and visible attributes → a rectangle around the text, following its rotation;
    • blocks, dimensions, multileaders and tables → the pieces of an exploded copy (texts in the pieces get a rectangle, attribute definitions are skipped);
    • hatches → their boundary, recreated with -HATCHEDIT;
    • images, wipeouts and OLE frames → a rectangle on their bounding box;
    • points, construction lines and rays are ignored.
  2. With a gap tolerance, every curve of the boundary set is replaced by bands of the gap width (rectangles along its straight pieces, its curved pieces divided every half gap). The bands are made regions and united (UNION), so they no longer overlap, and the united regions are exploded to their outlines: BOUNDARY traces those reliably.
  3. A temporary rectangular frame is drawn around the extents of the boundary set, and the view zooms on it (BOUNDARY only analyses what is on screen).
  4. -BOUNDARY runs with island detection on the boundary set, from a point between the objects and the frame. It creates the outline of the frame and the outline of each island, i.e. of each group of objects. With a gap tolerance, each outline is then offset inwards by half the gap.
  5. The outline of the frame and every temporary object are deleted, and the previous view is restored. When no outline was found, it is tried again with a larger gap (see Gap Tolerance).
  6. Every AutoCAD command is run with command-s (AutoCAD 2015 and later): a command that fails, e.g. BOUNDARY finding no closed area, leaves nothing pending, so the next tries still run.

Using It In An App

(Remote>>Dyl:Shrinkwrap ss) returns a selection set of the new polylines, or nil (it starts with a gap of 0). (Remote>>Dyl:ShrinkwrapGap ss gap) starts with the gap tolerance gap (drawing units). Both try larger gaps when no outline is found and print the gap used. They are used by the Shrinkwrap drawing method of Quick Draw.

Known Limitations

  • Works in the plane of the current UCS: objects must be flat and parallel to it.
  • Objects that do not touch or overlap give separate outlines, unless they are closer than the gap tolerance; gaps smaller than HPGAPTOL are closed.
  • Each automatic try with a larger gap takes as long as the first one: a large selection that needs many tries is slow. A larger gap also rounds the corners of the outline more.
  • Text rectangles are tight around the characters; mirrored or oblique text and mtext columns are approximated.
  • Blocks that cannot be exploded (explode not allowed, MINSERT) are traced as they are, without the text they contain.
  • Large selections with many blocks are slower, since each block is exploded on a temporary copy.
