Months

Opened by The open button of Months, under Climates in the Simulation window

Program Description

Give a climate of the Simulation its twelve months, to see over a year whether the water that condenses in winter dries out.

How To Use

  1. In the Simulation window, pick a climate and click the square button beside Months.
  2. Type the mean temperature and humidity of each month, then OK.
  3. Click Monthly.

Good To Know

  • Take the climate normals of the nearest weather station for outside.
  • A climate has all twelve months, or none.
