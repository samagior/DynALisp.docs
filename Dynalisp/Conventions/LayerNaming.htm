Description

DynALisp names its layers as the AIA CAD Layer Guidelines of the United States National CAD Standard (NCS), version 5, name them. A layer name is made of fields divided by dashes: who the content belongs to (the discipline), what it is (the major and minor groups) and its phase (the status). A-Wall-Full-E is an architectural wall, full height, existing to remain.

The layer tools read the names that way: Layer Transfer replaces one field of a name, Layer Phase reads and changes the status, the layer keys add the active phase to their layer, the layer filters group the layers by field, and Make Layers builds the names from their fields. A layer named another way still works in AutoCAD, but these tools leave it as it is.

Layer Name Format

A name has up to five fields, always in this order, each one divided from the next by a dash (-):

FieldCharactersRequiredExampleWhat it says
Discipline designator1 or 2YesA, ADThe subject of the layer: architectural, structural, electrical...
Major group4YesWallThe building system or element
Minor group 14NoFullDefines the major group further
Minor group 24NoDimsDefines the minor group further
Status1NoNThe phase of the work

From the shortest name to the longest:

NameFields
A-WallDiscipline, major group
AD-WallDiscipline of two letters (architectural demolition), major group
A-Wall-Full… one minor group
A-Wall-Full-Dims… two minor groups
A-Wall-Full-Dims-N… and the status
  • The discipline designator and the major group are always there; the minor groups and the status are optional. A minor group that defines the major group comes right after it.
  • Any major group can follow any discipline designator, and any minor group any major group, as long as each code keeps its meaning: S-Wall-Conc and A-Wall-Conc are both valid.
  • Pick the fields a project uses and keep them the same for every layer of the project.

Spelling In DynALisp

  • The NCS writes its names in capitals (A-WALL-FULL-N). DynALisp writes the discipline designator in capitals and each group with a capital and then lower case: A-Wall-Full-N, AS-Ctrl, A-Detl-Thin. It reads better in the layer list and on the command line.
  • AutoCAD does not tell the case of a layer name apart: A-WALL-FULL-N and A-Wall-Full-N are the same layer, and the DynALisp tools compare the fields without their case. A drawing named in capitals works as it is.
  • A group always has 4 characters. A shorter code is filled with ~ (Air~, Gas~, Top~), never with spaces.
  • A code of your own (one the lists do not have) is 4 letters, digits or ~, and must not take a meaning that the lists already give to another code. Note it in the standards of the project.

Minor Groups

Four characters each, at most two in a name, that define the major group further: A-Wall-Full (full height), A-Wall-Prht (partial height), A-Door-Iden (door tags), A-Wall-Full-Patt (the hatch of the full height walls). The list DynALisp reads is Modules\Dynalisp\Res\AEC\Standard\Minor.csv; Res\Layer\Standard\Minor.csv holds the same codes with the color and lineweight Make Layers gives them.

Status (Phase)

The last field, one character, gives the phase of the work. A layer without it is unphased. The codes DynALisp knows are those of Modules\Dynalisp\Res\AEC\Standard\Phase.csv:

  • The same object keeps its name in every phase, only the status changes: A-Wall-Full-E, A-Wall-Full-D, A-Wall-Full-N. That is what lets Layer Transfer move objects from one phase to another, and auto-phase put the new objects in the active phase.
  • A phase layer that DynALisp creates gets the phase color: 2# for existing to demolish, 20# for existing to remain (the last digit of the color of the unphased layer, which gives the lineweight of the pen table, is kept).
  • Use the letters or the numbers in a project, not both.

Other Names

  • 0, Defpoints and the layers of the xrefs (Plan|A-Wall) are never phased or transferred.
  • Some layers of the AutoCAD Architecture layer key style that DynALisp follows have three minor groups (G-Anno-Stnd-Nplt-Scrn, see AEC Layer Overrides). The NCS allows two, but the tools read them all the same: every 4-character field between the major group and the status is a minor group.
  • A name with a field of another length (A-Walls, Hatch) is not read as an NCS name: the Level transfers of Layer Transfer leave it as it is, and the wildcards of the layer filters may miss it.

ISO 13567

The NCS can be used in conceptual conformance with ISO 13567 (Organization and naming of layers for CAD). DynALisp does not check it; a project that needs it also follows these rules:

  • No major group Anno: annotation goes in a minor group kept for the annotation codes only (A-Wall-Text-N).
  • A minor group used for the drawing views (Elev, Sect...) holds no other code.
  • Every name of the project has the same fields and the same length: a field not used is filled with underscores (A-Wall-Elev-____-N).
  • The status holds the letters or the phase numbers, not both.
  • The format of the project is described in a layer naming definition file, as ISO 13567-3 asks.

Source

AIA CAD Layer Guidelines, United States National CAD Standard, version 5, National Institute of Building Sciences, 2011. Its appendices list every discipline designator, major group, minor group and status, and the layers most used by each discipline.
