Layer Pen Colors

Function Syntax DYL-LAYERPENCOLORS
Licence GNU General Public License v3

Program Description

Switches the layers from the 255 colors of the Dyl Standard to a small set of pen colors, 1 to 7, chosen from the current lineweight of each layer. A few colors and layer names are kept or get a color of their own (rules in Res\Layer\PenColors.csv). The colors of the layers are saved in the drawing, so the switch can be undone at any time with Restore.

Commands

CommandNameDescription
DYL-LAYERPENCOLORSLayer Pen ColorsSwitches the layer colors to the pen colors 1 to 7 from their lineweight, or restores their saved colors.
PST-LAYERCOLORSTOPENSLayer Colors To PensSets the layer colors to the pen colors 1 to 7 from their lineweight (30 for 0.13), saving their colors.
PST-LAYERCOLORSRESTORELayer Colors RestoreGives the layers back the colors saved when they were switched to the pen colors.

Rules

The layers matching a rule of Res\Layer\PenColors.csv are not switched by their lineweight. The first rule matching a layer applies. Layers are layer names (wildcards, any case), Colors the current colors (wildcards: 20# is 200 to 209), and Color the color given to the layer, empty to keep its color as is. Edit the file in Excel. Your copy is saved in User\ (see Dyl-UserFiles).

Known Limitations

  • Layers with lineweight Default or 2.00 mm (no pen color) are not switched. Set their lineweight first, for example from their color with Pst-LayerWeightByColor (LWC).
  • Xref layers are not changed.
  • Only the layers are changed: objects with their own color are not.
  • A layer color changed by hand while on pen colors is replaced by the saved one on Restore.

Command Names And Aliases

Each program has one main command starting with Dyl-. The short names are its aliases: they run the same command, and can be added, renamed or removed in Dyl-Suite (saved in Resources\Aliases.csv).
