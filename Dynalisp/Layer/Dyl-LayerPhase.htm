Layer Phase

Function Syntax DYL-LAYERPHASE (PH), PH-E, PH-D, PH-N, PH-F, PH-T, PH-NONE, PH-NEXT, PH-AUTO
Requires AutoCAD 2025 or later for Windows
Licence GNU General Public License v3

Program Description

Draw each phase of a renovation on its own layers, and switch phase in one command. A phased layer ends with a dash and its phase: A-Wall-Full-E for an existing wall to remain, A-Wall-Full-D to demolish, A-Wall-Full-N for new work (see Layer Naming).

Each drawing has an active phase, shown in the status bar and in the ribbon. With auto-phase on, everything you draw goes to the active phase: copy an existing wall while the phase is new work, and the copy lands on A-Wall-Full-N.

How To Use

  1. Pick the active phase – type PH-E (existing), PH-D (demolition), PH-N (new work), PH-F (future) or PH-T (temporary), or pick it in the Phase drop-down of the ribbon. The current layer moves to that phase with it: A-Wall-Full-E becomes A-Wall-Full-N. PH-NEXT goes to the next phase, PH-NONE clears it.
  2. Turn auto-phase on – type PH-AUTO, pick Auto-phase On in the ribbon, or check Auto-phase new objects in Dyl-Settings. It needs the DynALisp reactors on in Dyl-Settings; the status bar then shows [Auto].
  3. Draw as usual – each object you create goes to the layer of its phase, made when the drawing does not have it yet. The layer keys give their layer in the active phase too: QD-BRICK draws on A-Detl-Medm-E in existing.
  4. Show or hide a phase – type PH to open the Layer Phases window (below).

Layer Phases Window

  • Phase list – each phase with its number of layers and objects in the drawing, and whether its layers are Shown, Hidden or Partial. The active phase is marked >; Unphased counts the layers without a phase. Select several phases with Ctrl or Shift.
  • Double-click a phase to make it the active phase (Unphased: no active phase).
  • Show / Hide – shows the selected phases, or hides them when they are all shown. Hide hides them whatever their state. Isolate phase shows them and hides the other phases (never the unphased layers). The drawing updates behind the window.
  • Layer keys Editor – opens the Layer Keys window, then comes back here.

Ribbon

The Drawing Behaviors panel of the DynALisp tab has the Layer Phase button (the window) and two drop-downs: Auto-phase (On, Off; On (reactors off) while the DynALisp reactors are off) and Phase (the active phase of the current drawing). Like the Layer drop-down of AutoCAD, they always show the current state. The drop-downs need AutoCAD 2025 or later (see Ribbon): in an older version, use the PH-* aliases and the status bar.

Good To Know

  • Layers 0, Defpoints and the layers of xrefs never get a phase; objects on a layer without a phase stay there, except on the layer of a layer key.
  • Editing commands leave their objects where they are: TRIM, BREAK, FILLET, JOIN, EXPLODE, pasting and UNDO never move anything to the active phase.
  • A new phase layer copies its source layer, with the phase colors of Layer Transfer for demolition and existing.
  • The command line says how many objects moved, and warns when their layer is off or frozen.

Known Limitations

  • Auto-phase moves the objects just after their command: the first U puts them back on the layer they were drawn on, the second one undoes the command.
  • The status bar shows the phase only when MODEMACRO is not used by something else.
  • After an UNDO that brings back a former phase, the drop-down and the status bar show it only at the next change of phase, or when the drawing is made current again.
  • MIRROR with Erase source objects moves the mirrored objects to the active phase, as any copy.
