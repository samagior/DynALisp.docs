Layer Color Weight

Function Syntax DYL-LAYERCOLORWEIGHT
Source File Modules\Dynalisp\Apps\Layer\Dyl-LayerColorWeight.lsp
Licence GNU General Public License v3

Program Description

Makes the layers follow the Dyl Standard plot style (Res\Layer\Dyl Standard.pdf, Dyl Standard.ctb), where the color of a layer gives its plotted lineweight. It either sets the lineweight of each layer from its color, or the other way around, sets the color of each layer from its lineweight, keeping its hue.

Commands

CommandNameDescription
DYL-LAYERCOLORWEIGHTLayer Color WeightMatches the lineweight of the layers with their color, or their color with their lineweight (Dyl Standard).
PST-LAYERWEIGHTBYCOLORLayer Lineweight By ColorSets the lineweight of the layers from their color (Dyl Standard: color ##0 = 0.13, ##1 = 0.18...).
PST-LAYERCOLORBYWEIGHTLayer Color By LineweightSets the last digit of the layer colors from their lineweight, keeping their hue (Dyl Standard).

The Standard

ColorsLineweight
##0 (10, 20 ... 240)0.13 mm (prints black)
1 and ##10.18 mm
2 and ##20.25 mm
3 and ##30.35 mm
4 and ##40.50 mm
5 and ##50.70 mm

7 and ##71.40 mm

##9, 8, 9, 250 to 2550.13 mm (greys)

How It Works

  1. With DYL-LAYERCOLORWEIGHT, choose Lineweight (default, the lineweight follows the color) or Color (the color follows the lineweight). The two child commands skip this question.
  2. Choose All (default, every layer of the drawing) or Select (the layers of the objects you select).
  3. Lineweight from color: each layer takes the lineweight of its color in the table above.
  4. Color from lineweight: a color from 10 to 249 keeps its tens (its hue) and takes the last digit of its lineweight: color 11 with 0.35 mm becomes 13. A pen color 1 to 6 goes to its hue: red 1 with 0.35 mm becomes 13. Black (7) and the greys go to the pen color of the lineweight: 7 with 0.35 mm becomes 3. A lineweight that is not in the standard (0.30 mm) takes the nearest one (0.35 mm). A layer that already matches is left as is, so a dark grey ##9 layer at 0.13 mm stays dark grey.
  5. The changes are one undo step. The command reports how many layers changed, how many already matched, and lists the layers not changed.

Known Limitations

  • Layers in true color or from a color book are not changed, nor the layers of xrefs.
  • Color from lineweight leaves out the layers whose lineweight is Default, and the black or grey layers at 0.13 mm or 2.00 mm (no pen color has these lineweights).
  • Only the layers are changed: objects with their own color or lineweight are not.
