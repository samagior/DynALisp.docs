Text Fix

Function Syntax DYL-TEXTFIX (TEXTFIX)
Works with Assistant – signs you in to Claude
Requires Claude Code signed in with your own Claude account, an internet connection

Program Description

Have Claude proofread the texts of your drawing: spelling, grammar and accents, in their own language. Terms, units and codes stay as they are.

The selected texts are sent to Anthropic. Do not use it on texts that must stay on your computer.

How To Use

  1. Once – turn on Text Fix in Dyl-Suite, and sign in with the Assistant.
  2. Select the texts and type TEXTFIX.
  3. For each correction, answer Oui, Non, Tous or Quitter.
  4. One U undoes them all.

Known Limitations

  • Not the attributes of blocks.
  • The questions are in French.
