Rebuild Patterns

Function Syntax DYL-REBUILDPAT (RPAT), OPENPATS
Requires AutoCAD 2025 or later for Windows
Licence GNU General Public License v3

Program Description

A drawing from someone else uses hatch patterns you do not have? Get them back from its hatches, to use them in your own.

How To Use

  1. Type RPAT, select hatches or press Enter for all.
  2. The missing patterns are added. OPENPATS opens their folder.

Known Limitations

  • A pattern rebuilt can differ a little from the original.
  • Solid fills and gradients need nothing to rebuild.
