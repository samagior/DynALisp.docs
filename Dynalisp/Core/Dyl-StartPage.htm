Start Page

Works with Diagnostics – runs for Report a problem
Settings – turns it on (Start page)
Requires The Start tab of AutoCAD turned on

Description

Your work at a glance, in the Start tab of AutoCAD: start a drawing on the left, your pinned work, your time and your recent drawings in the middle, the tasks of your projects on the right.

How To Use

  1. In Dyl-Settings, turn on Start page, and Active time to count your time. Click Save.
  2. Start a drawing with New, a template or Open....
  3. Right-click a recent drawing to Pin it, its sheet set or its project.
  4. Click a drawing to see its days in the graph. Double-click it to open it.
  5. Type a task under a project and press Enter. Check it when done.

The Page

  • New, Open and the templates – start or open a drawing.
  • Pinned – up to 6 drawings, sheet sets or projects you keep at hand.
  • Active time – a square per day, darker for more time. Click a day to list its drawings.
  • Recent – your last drawings. Search finds one by name. Columns sets the size of the cards.
  • Projects – the tasks of each pinned project. Right-click a project and check Keep tasks with the project to share them with your team.
  • The foot of the page – your version, Settings, Report a problem and Docs.

Active Time

A drawing counts while you work in it. A pause of more than 3 minutes does not count. Your time stays on your computer.

Previews

Right-click a drawing to change its picture:

  • Choose preview picture... – any image, framed in the Preview Picture window.
  • Set preview area... – pick two corners in the drawing. The picture follows each time you save.
  • Use its own preview – back to the picture AutoCAD keeps.

Report a Problem

Say what happened, then click Open the issue. DynALisp adds a report of your setup and opens it on GitHub, where you read it and submit it. Press Ctrl+V in the issue to attach the report. Nothing is sent until you submit.
